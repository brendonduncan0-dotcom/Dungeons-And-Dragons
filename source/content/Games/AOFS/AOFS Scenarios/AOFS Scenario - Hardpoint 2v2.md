---
title: Scenario - Hardpoint 2v2
---



>[!summary] Objectives
> Before deployment, players have to set up 5 objective markers, as shown in the mission diagram. 
> 
> The hardpoint starts at objective marker 1, the players then randomly determine the remaining sequence. Only one hardpoint is ever active at a time, deactivated locations cannot be reactivated and have no effect for the remainder of the game. 
> 
> At the end of round 1, the Hardpoint deactivates at location 1 and reactivates at the next objective marker in sequence. It continues changing to the next location at the end of each subsequent round.
> 
> At the end of each round, if a unit is within 3” of a marker whilst enemies aren’t, then the objective is seized. 
> 

>[!summary] Deployment
> One player from each team rolls-off, and the winner picks their teams two connected deployment zones first, with their opponents taking the opposite. Then the teams alternate, placing one unit for each player within their deployment zone, starting with the team who won the deployment roll-off.

>[!summary] First Turn
> The team that has won the deployment roll-off gets the first turn.
> 
> Within a round, teams alternate activations: the team going first activates one of their two players (their choice which), then the other team activates on of theirs, then the first team's remaining player, then the second team's remaining player.
> 
> Priority to go first each round is based on the opposite team of who acted acted last.

>[!summary] Scoring
> At the end of each round teams get 5 VP if they control the active hardpoint.
> 
> The game ends after 4 rounds, and the team that scored most VPs wins.

>[!summary] Side Missions
> At the end of the game teams get 2 VPs for each side-mission they completed: 
> - Destroy a unit with a higher point cost than the attacker.
> - Have a unit from each player on your team within 3" of the same hardpoint simultaneously. 
> - Have every friendly Hero alive at the end of the game.
> - Be the first team to control a Hardpoint location this game.
> - Control the active hardpoint for 3 or more consecutive rounds.

>[!summary] Special Objectives
>In addition to winning/losing players may earn a bonus 10 pts by completing special objectives. 
>
>Players roll for a random bonus objective at the start of the game (See [[AOFS Special Objectives]]).
