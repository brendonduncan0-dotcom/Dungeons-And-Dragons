# Curse and Disease Removal

This note defines the procedure when a character needs a curse or disease removed through guild services.

## Trigger

This event activates when:
- A character contracts a disease and seeks treatment at [[The Bubbling Cauldron]] (Potion Shop)
- A character is cursed and seeks removal at [[Bilgo Bangwiz Bazaar]] (Wizard Shop)

Simple diseases or minor curses handled by low-level spell effects (e.g. *Lesser Restoration*) during a normal session do not trigger this procedure. This event is for conditions requiring the guild's specialist services.

## Procedure

Guild removal services are a straightforward paid transaction — no materials quest required.

**1. Diagnosis.** The relevant specialist (Aster for disease, Bilgo for curses) examines the character and identifies the severity tier of the condition.
- Disease: Common / Rare / Exotic Magical (see [[The Bubbling Cauldron]] for pricing)
- Curse: Basic / Powerful / Ancient or Legendary (see [[Bilgo Bangwiz Bazaar]] for pricing)

**2. Payment.** The party pays the listed gold cost for that severity tier, per the relevant shop's price list.

**3. Treatment.** The condition is removed on payment. No materials collection, no dedicated one-shot.

**Exception — Ancient/Legendary curses:** Bilgo's price list lists these as "consultation required" rather than a flat fee. At the DM's discretion, a sufficiently severe or story-relevant curse in this tier can still escalate into a short quest or negotiation if it serves the session — but this is an optional narrative choice, not the default procedure.

## Notes for the DM

- **Scale the severity tier to the narrative threat**, not just flavor — a disease picked up from a minor scuffle should be Common, not Exotic.
- **Affordability is the pressure point**, not a quest requirement. If the party can't cover the cost, that's a roleplay/financial complication (a loan from the guild, a favor owed, a discount negotiated) — consistent with the campaign's resource-attrition difficulty design.
- **The condition remains active until paid for** — this still gives the party a reason to prioritize it, just without forcing a session detour.

> [!tip] DM Note
> This procedure was updated to match the priced services established for [[The Bubbling Cauldron]] and [[Bilgo Bangwiz Bazaar]] — it previously required a dedicated materials-collection one-shot, but that's been replaced with straight gold cost for consistency across DMs. If a particular curse or disease has strong story potential, nothing stops a DM from building a one-shot around its source anyway — just don't make it a prerequisite for the cure.
