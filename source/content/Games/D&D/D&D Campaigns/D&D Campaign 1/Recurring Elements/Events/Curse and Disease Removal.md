# Curse and Disease Removal

This note defines the procedure when a character needs a curse or disease removed through guild services.

## Trigger

This event activates when:
- A character contracts a disease and seeks removal via the [[The Guild Headquarters|Potion Shop]]
- A character is cursed and seeks removal via the [[The Guild Headquarters|Wizard Shop]]

Simple diseases or minor curses handled by low-level spell effects (e.g. *Lesser Restoration*) during a normal session do not trigger this procedure. This event is for conditions requiring the guild's specialist services.

## Procedure

Guild removal services are not a gold transaction. When a character requests this service, the following happens:

**1. Assessment.** The relevant guild specialist (apothecary or wizard) examines the character and identifies the specific materials required for the removal ritual or cure formula.

**2. Contract issued.** The guild issues the party a contract to collect the required materials. This becomes the hook for a dedicated one-shot.

**3. One-shot.** The party undertakes the materials-collection mission. The specific quest is generated by the DM running that session using the `/new-oneshot` command, with the material requirements as the stated objective.

**4. Removal.** On successful return with materials, the guild performs the removal at no additional gold cost beyond the one-shot reward structure. If the party returns without all required materials, the removal cannot be completed and a follow-up contract may be needed.

## Notes for the DM

- **Generate the one-shot promptly.** The afflicted character should not be sitting on a debilitating condition through multiple unrelated sessions. Aim to run the removal one-shot within one or two sessions of the trigger.
- **Scale the materials quest** to the severity of the condition. A common disease might require one component from a local monster; a powerful ancient curse might require multiple sessions.
- **The condition remains active** during the materials quest — this is intentional. The party is motivated to complete it quickly.

> [!tip] DM Note
> These quests are an opportunity to introduce new locations, factions, or lore tied to the source of the curse or disease. A curse from a specific villain might require materials found in their home territory, giving the party context about a larger threat. Don't treat it as a fetch quest — give the material source a reason for existing.
