# Bottom of the Barrel

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Warm lanterns hang from the beams over oversized tables built for sharing stories and exaggerating accomplishments. The walls are a museum of pirate memorabilia, retired gear, maps, monster trophies, and tankards signed by famous adventurers. A stone fireplace dominates one wall, and the smell of breakfast fills the room — because there's only one thing on the menu, at every hour of every day. Near the door, a woman with a faded skull-and-crossbones tattoo refills mugs and somehow already knows your name.

## Overview

The headquarters' tavern, inn, breakfast establishment, and informal quest hub. Part of the **Shopping District**, within easy reach of the [[The Guild Headquarters|castle's main courtyard]]. Functionally the campaign's social home base — this is where downtime roleplay, guild gossip, and the **Quest Board** (see below) live.

## Proprietor: Captain Briney "Bones" Blackwake

A former pirate captain who spent decades sailing the seas of Faerûn through treasure hunting, sea monsters, mutiny attempts, and poor financial decisions — and then, to everyone's surprise, retired into running a tavern extremely well.

> "Life's easier after a hot meal. If it ain't easier after that, have another hot meal."

**Personality:** Friendly, approachable, and carries the effortless authority of someone who's survived more disasters than most people can imagine. Knows the names and stories of almost every regular, gives unexpectedly wise advice, and always seems to know when a quest has gone badly or when someone needs encouragement — though she'd rather nobody made a fuss about it. Quietly covers the tab for struggling adventurers and refuses to admit she's sentimental.

**The tattoo:** Bones has an ordinary skull-and-crossbones tattoo on her upper arm. Whenever a fight breaks out in the tavern, she simply rolls up her sleeve and shows it — and the fight stops, fast. Nobody is sure why. Nobody wants to test the theory.

## The Breakfast Policy

Bones believes breakfast is the best meal of the day, and pirates rarely get a proper one — an injustice she now corrects unilaterally. **Breakfast is the only thing on the menu, served all day, every day, no exceptions.** Ask for lunch, dinner, or dessert and you get breakfast, sweet breakfast, or more breakfast respectively. She sees no issue with this.

**Signature Drink — The Scurvy Slayer (5 sp):** A gigantic tankard of orange, lemon, lime, grapefruit, and "more citrus." *"If this doesn't cure your scurvy, you're probably already dead."*

**Celebration Package (5 gp):** For parties returning from a successful contract — reserved table, complimentary breakfast platter, and the adventure gets added to the guild's Story Wall.

**Adventurer Story Wall:** Members can submit their own adventure stories for the wall. Accuracy is highly variable; entertainment value is not.

## The Quest Board

Mounted outside the tavern entrance, this is where most public contracts are posted first. Postings may include monster hunts, escort contracts, missing persons, ruin exploration, bounty notices, investigation requests, guild requests, and odd jobs. More sensitive or higher-stakes missions bypass the board and go directly to a party instead.

> [!tip] DM Note — Use this as the contract hook
> The campaign's **Always Include: Guild contract framing** rule says every session opens with a contract. The Quest Board is the natural in-fiction delivery mechanism for public/lower-stakes jobs — describe the board, let the party pick a posting, and you have your session hook with zero extra setup. Save direct-delivery contracts (an officer pulling the party aside) for jobs that need to feel personal, urgent, or secretive.
