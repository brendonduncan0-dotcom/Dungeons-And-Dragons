# Silvers Blades

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The contrast with the forge across the hall is immediate — cool, quiet, and almost unnervingly clean. Blades hang in perfect symmetry like museum pieces. At the centre of the room, a basin of moonlit water glows soft silver-blue, its steam drifting blue-tinted through the air. A tall, impeccably dressed man glances up from a half-finished blade, takes in your stance and the state of your gear in one practiced look, and says nothing yet — but you can tell he's already found something to comment on.

## Overview

The headquarters' weaponsmith, bladesmith, and custom arms dealer. Part of the **Shopping District**, within easy reach of the [[The Guild Headquarters|castle's main courtyard]] — directly across the hall from [[Steele's Armory]].

## Proprietor: Silver

A Water Genasi, tall and slender, impeccably dressed in elegant robes and tailored coats with silver embroidery, flowing water-like hair, and a permanent expression of mild disappointment. First-time visitors often mistake him for a noble, scholar, or wealthy merchant rather than a smith — but he's widely regarded as one of the finest weaponsmiths in Faerûn.

> "A weapon is merely a conversation. Whether it becomes a discussion or an argument depends entirely upon the wielder."

His forge centres on a basin of enchanted moonlit water; he shapes and refines metal using magically generated steam with extraordinary precision, rather than Steele's open flame.

**Personality:** Polite, elegant, professionally precise — and incapable of ignoring a flaw. A crooked grip, poor posture, a poorly maintained weapon, a smudge on a display case — Silver notices everything and will comment on it, usually with perfectly delivered dry sarcasm. He corrects customers' grips unasked, judges adventurers by how well they maintain their gear, and has memorised the exact location of every item in the shop. Beneath the sarcasm he genuinely cares about the quality of his work and his customers — though he'd never admit the second part aloud.

**Notable quirk:** Silver insists he has no interest whatsoever in [[Steele's Armory|Steele]]. He also somehow always knows exactly where Steele is. A coincidence, surely.

> [!tip] DM Note
> Silver's unsolicited but always-correct critiques (bad grip, poorly maintained gear, wrong weapon for the job) work the same way as Steele's — a blunt, in-fiction way to telegraph mechanical danger before a fight, consistent with the campaign's "no surprise lethality" rule. The Steele/Silver slow-burn is a standing background bit the party can poke at for comic relief during downtime; it doesn't need to resolve quickly, and probably shouldn't.
