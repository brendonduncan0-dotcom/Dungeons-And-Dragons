# The Guild Headquarters

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The headquarters rises above the surrounding land like a small city unto itself — towers, courtyards, covered walkways, and the constant low hum of people who live and work and train here. The gate crest bears the guild's emblem. Inside, you know where everything is. It smells like stone and coal smoke and something faintly magical. It smells like home.

## Overview

The guild headquarters — home of the **Faerûn Adventure League** — is a large self-sufficient castle complex that serves as the party's permanent home base. It sits in the outskirts of Waterdeep, on the Sword Coast: close enough to the city for contracts and supply runs, far enough to have its own grounds and identity distinct from Waterdeep proper.

It functions as a full institution — member quarters, training halls, administrative offices, and a complete range of services. Arriving here between sessions means the party can resupply, seek information, recover, and interact with the broader guild community.

The castle's internal layout, named rooms, and resident personalities are to be discovered and documented during play. Add new notes and wikilinks to this file as the party explores.
