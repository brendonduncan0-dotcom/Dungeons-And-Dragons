# Missy's Magnificent Miscellaneous Merchandise

> [!info] Read Aloud
> A hand-painted sign creaks above the door: *"Good gear. Good sense. Safe travels!"* Inside, every shelf is crammed with rope, lanterns, bedrolls, and a hundred small useful things, and the whole shop smells faintly of leather and candle wax. A sleepy white cat watches from a basket near the ceiling. Behind the counter, an orange tabby — standing upright, sleeves rolled to the elbow — is doing something complicated with a ball of blue yarn and not looking up yet.

## Overview

The headquarters' general store — everyday adventuring supplies sold by a uniquely transformed proprietor. Part of the **Shopping District**, within easy reach of the [[The Guild Headquarters|castle's main courtyard]].

## Proprietor: Missy Whiskerpaws

An orange tabby cat who, years ago, accidentally drank an experimental transformation potion. She now stands roughly 4 feet tall, can walk upright or on all fours depending on her mood, has opposable thumbs, and speaks Common fluently — but remains unmistakably feline in appearance.

**Personality:** Treats every customer like a grandchild whether they want it or not — slips extra biscuits into packs, fusses over warm clothing, insists everyone take a spare length of rope ("you never know"). Despite the warmth, she's wonderfully chaotic: naps on the front counter mid-business-hours, chases mice through important transactions, knocks merchandise off shelves while maintaining unbroken eye contact, and will flatly refuse to sell to adventurers she judges to be making "poor life choices." Regulars suspect she knows far more about her customers than she lets on — nobody knows how.

## Stock

Everyday adventuring supplies — Missy calls it "everything sensible":

- Backpacks and travel packs
- Rope, twine, and climbing gear
- Bedrolls and tents
- Lanterns, oil, and candles
- Waterskins and mess kits
- Rations and trail food
- Maps and journals
- Basic tools and repair kits
- Fishing supplies
- Soap, sewing kits, and travel necessities

She refuses to stock magic items, weapons, armour, or anything likely to cause "unnecessary explosions."

> [!tip] DM Note
> Missy's uncanny knack for knowing what a party will need makes her a natural source of subtle foreshadowing — have her press a specific item on the party (extra rope, a lantern, a particular tool) right before a session where that exact item becomes relevant, without ever breaking the fiction. This supports the campaign's rule that deadly encounters must always be telegraphed in advance.
