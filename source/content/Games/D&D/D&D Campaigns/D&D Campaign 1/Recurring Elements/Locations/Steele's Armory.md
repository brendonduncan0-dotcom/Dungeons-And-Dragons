# Steele's Armory

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Heat rolls off the forge before you're even through the door. An ancient furnace burns at the heart of the workshop, its molten light spilling through vents and washing rows of armour stands in orange. Hammer rings against steel in a steady rhythm. The smith doesn't look up — broad-shouldered, soot-streaked, eyes smouldering faintly like banked embers — until the metal in his tongs is ready.

## Overview

The headquarters' armoury, shieldworks, and master smithing shop. Part of the **Shopping District**, within easy reach of the [[The Guild Headquarters|castle's main courtyard]] — directly across the hall from [[Silvers Blades]].

## Proprietor: Steele

A Fire Genasi, well over six feet tall, broad-shouldered, soot-stained, with smouldering ember-like eyes — looks as though carved from volcanic rock and given life by a stubborn fire elemental. Permanently warm to the touch, heavily muscled, covered in scars from a lifetime at the forge. Appears to be in his thirties but carries himself like someone who has worked every day of his life.

> "Armour won't save your life. Wearing it properly might."

The forge itself is powered by the captured heart of a Fire Elemental.

**Personality:** A man of almost no words — a grunt, a nod, maybe one sentence if you're lucky. Despite the silence, his judgement on armour is trusted absolutely: if he says a breastplate won't fit, it won't; if he says a shield is unbalanced, it is; if he says you're not strong enough for a suit of armour, you probably aren't. He asks every customer to lift something heavy before they leave, critiques posture unprompted, can spot armour weaknesses at a glance, and remembers every piece he's ever crafted. He almost never smiles.

**Notable quirk:** Steele is frequently seen glancing across the hall toward [[Silvers Blades]]. He denies it every time. Nobody believes him.

## Stock & Services

**Light Armour:** Padded (5 gp), Leather (10 gp), Studded Leather (45 gp)

**Medium Armour:** Hide (10 gp), Chain Shirt (50 gp), Scale Mail (50 gp), Breastplate (400 gp), Half Plate (750 gp)

**Heavy Armour:** Ring Mail (30 gp), Chain Mail (75 gp), Splint (200 gp), Plate (1,500 gp)

**Shields:** Shield (10 gp), Reinforced Shield (50 gp), Decorative Shield (100 gp), Custom Heraldic Shield (150 gp+)

**Mount Equipment:** Saddles, barding, harnesses, travel tack, custom mount armour — priced by creature size and materials.

**Repair Services:** Minor Armour Repair (10 gp), Major Armour Repair (50 gp), Shield Repair (15 gp), Battle Damage Restoration (quote required)

**Custom Commissions:** Custom armour sets, family heirloom restoration, tournament armour, ceremonial armour, custom shields, mount barding, adventuring equipment upgrades

**Forge Upgrades (by request):** Engraving, heraldry, decorative filigree, reinforced joints, lightweight modifications, fire-resistant treatments

> "Good armour is expensive. Bad armour is more expensive."
