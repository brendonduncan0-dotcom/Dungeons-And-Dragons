# Bilgo Bangwiz Bazaar

> [!info] Read Aloud
> A hand-painted sign over the door reads *"Bilgo Bangwiz Bazaar (Death Not Guaranteed)."* Inside, books are stacked on books stacked on shelves — some clearly load-bearing. Wands, scrolls, unlabelled potions, and things that may once have been alive crowd every surface. A kobold in scorched purple robes is reading aloud from a smoking book titled *Advanced Pyrotheurgy for Amateurs*, grinning with far too many teeth, while a terrified-looking customer is dragged out the door by a much calmer lizardfolk colleague.

## Overview

The headquarters' wizard shop — arcane supplies, spell scrolls, magical items, curse removal, and the guild's death insurance program. Part of the **Shopping District**, within easy reach of the [[The Guild Headquarters|castle's main courtyard]].

## Proprietor: Bilgo Bangwiz

A kobold — that much everyone agrees on. His exact age, origin, and how he learned so much magic are all unknown, including to Bilgo himself. He has survived for far longer than most kobolds should and seems to have genuinely forgotten that death is supposed to be permanent. This explains a great deal.

> "I've exploded loads of times. Statistically speaking, you're probably fine."

**Personality:** Constant motion, constant talk, constant experimentation — the energy of a wizard who drank too much coffee decades ago and never came down. Refuses to throw anything away ("What if I need it later?"), frequently loses items while holding them, has invented multiple organisational systems and understands none of them. Customers regularly leave more confused than they arrived, or having accidentally bought something they didn't intend to. Bilgo considers both outcomes a success.

## Death Insurance

The cornerstone of Bilgo's business and the source of the shop's slogan. This is the guild's confirmed **retainer-based death insurance** service referenced in [[Player Death]] (Step 1 — Check Death Insurance).

For a fee, Bilgo collects a lock of hair and archives it. If the insured individual dies, Bilgo uses restorative magic and experimental arcane procedures to bring them back — usually.

**Package:**
- Initial Enrollment — 500 gp
- Annual Renewal — 100 gp (coverage lapses if unpaid, per [[Player Death]])

**Benefits:** Resurrection services, magical recovery assistance, priority restoration, complimentary tea during revival.

**Disclaimer:** Returned customers may experience slight personality changes, new food preferences, temporary memory gaps, unusual dreams, or mild existential confusion. Bilgo insists these are "mostly cosmetic." *(These line up neatly with the Resurrection Consequences described in [[Player Death]] Step 4 — use them as the specific roleplay flavor when a PC comes back through Bilgo's insurance.)*

## Stock & Other Services

**Spell Scrolls** (inventory changes daily): Level 1 (50 gp) through Level 9 (25,000 gp), doubling/escalating roughly by level. Availability not guaranteed.

**Arcane Components:** Common/rare spell components, monster parts, ritual supplies, arcane foci, component pouches, magical ink, enchanted paper — priced by rarity and whether Bilgo can actually locate them.

**Magical Items:** Common, uncommon, curious enchanted trinkets, experimental prototypes, questionable inventions. No refunds unless legally required.

**Remove Curse:** Basic Curse Removal (250 gp), Powerful Curse Removal (1,000 gp+), Ancient or Legendary Curses (consultation required). This is a straightforward paid service — once the party covers the listed cost for the diagnosed severity tier, the curse is lifted on the spot. See [[Curse and Disease Removal]] for the full procedure, including the optional exception for Ancient/Legendary cases.

**Arcane Identification:** Item Identification (50 gp), Complex Artifact Analysis (250 gp)

**Magical Consultation:** Arcane Advice (25 gp), Research Assistance (50 gp/hour)

**Custom Commissions:** Custom spell scrolls, magical research, enchanted items, arcane experimentation, curse removal, theoretical magical projects. Success rate varies; Bilgo won't give exact percentages.

> [!warning] Customer Notice (in-world)
> By entering the shop, customers acknowledge: unexpected magical effects may occur, spatial anomalies are possible, temporal anomalies are unlikely but not impossible, death is not guaranteed, and safety is also not guaranteed.
>
> "If everything goes according to plan, I've probably made a mistake somewhere."

> [!tip] DM Note
> Bilgo's "I've exploded loads of times" attitude makes him a great source of comic-relief danger that's never actually a surprise to the table — he can hand the party an item with an honestly-disclosed-but-absurd risk, which satisfies the campaign's "telegraph danger" rule while staying lighthearted. His hoarding habit ("what if I need it later?") is a built-in excuse for any plot-relevant magic item to plausibly already be somewhere in his shop, buried.
