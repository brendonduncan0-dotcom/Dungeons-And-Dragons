# The Bubbling Cauldron

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The shop is half apothecary, half greenhouse — living vines crawl the walls, mushrooms grow from enchanted logs, and flowers along the windowsill seem to turn as you pass. A mortar and pestle sit half-used on a cluttered workbench. An ageless elf with long silver hair looks up from a sprig of glowing leaves, smiles, and says nothing — but somewhere nearby, a fern rustles, as if it just told them something about you.

## Overview

The headquarters' potion shop, apothecary, and herbalist. Part of the **Shopping District**, within easy reach of the [[The Guild Headquarters|castle's main courtyard]]. Provides the guild's confirmed disease-removal service referenced in the [[The Guild Headquarters|Guild Headquarters]] services table.

## Proprietor: Aster

An ageless elf with no concept of gender, who identifies simply as a caretaker of life, growth, and healing. Has spent centuries studying botany, medicine, alchemy, and disease, and is one of the most knowledgeable healers affiliated with the Faerûn Adventure League.

> "Every ailment tells a story. Fortunately, I speak fluent root, leaf, petal, and fungus."

**Aster's gift:** Every plant in the shop constantly whispers information only Aster can hear — who's sick, stressed, injured, exhausted, or attempting to steal. Aster carries on full conversations with hanging ferns and occasionally pauses mid-sale to consult a flower's opinion. The plants are usually correct, and nobody has ever successfully stolen from the shop. Aster can often identify illnesses before symptoms become obvious, never forgets a face, and customarily serves tea before discussing business.
