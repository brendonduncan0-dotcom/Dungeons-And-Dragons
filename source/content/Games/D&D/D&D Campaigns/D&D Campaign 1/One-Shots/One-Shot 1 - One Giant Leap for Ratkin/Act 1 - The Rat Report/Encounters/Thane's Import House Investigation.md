# Thane's Import House Investigation

**Act:** 1
**Location:** [[Thane's Import House]]
**Encounter type:** Exploration

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The warehouse smells of sawdust and rope hemp — or it did, before most of the rope and lumber vanished. The remaining stock sits in disarray, as though carefully sorted through. Three ragged holes punch through the lower wall, each roughly the size of a dinner plate, edges chewed through brick. Near each hole, small precise marks are scratched into the mortar.

## Setup

Thane's warehouse was robbed over three consecutive nights. Entry points are unmistakably rodent-made (chewed brick) but large enough to haul significant material through, suggesting organisation and numbers. Nothing of obvious monetary value was taken — only hemp rope (30 metres), copper wire (a significant spool), and timber planks (a dozen). A character with a military or carpentry background will recognise this as exactly the material list for a large siege frame.

The symbols near the holes are small but precise — clearly a deliberate system, not random scratching.

[[Aldric Thane]] follows the party around describing the missing stock in exhaustive detail. Let him become background noise.

## Discoveries

| Check | DC | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Investigation | 10 | Entry holes were made from outside; drag marks show material was hauled outward into the alley |
| Investigation | 13 | The symbols near each hole are in slightly different styles — as if made by different authors at different times |
| Nature | 11 | Rat droppings and fur caught in the chewed edges. Definitely rats. Very large, organised rats. |
| Nature | 14 | Fresh paw prints in the alley dust lead directly toward the drainage grate in the alley floor |
| History | 15 | Rope, copper wire, and timber planks in these quantities suggest a tension-frame siege weapon — a ballista or similar |
| Arcana | 14 | One set of symbols carries a faint residual transmutation signature, as if something magically enhanced was briefly stored nearby |

## Objectives

- Confirm the culprits are rats (or at least rodents)
- Find the symbols and recognise them as a deliberate system
- Locate the drainage grate in the alley (the start of the trail leading to [[The Scratched Wall District]])

> [!tip] DM Note
> Let the party feel clever here. Even without the high-DC checks, the core facts — rats, symbols, drainage grate — should emerge from any thorough search. The History check about siege weapons is a satisfying payoff; consider letting any character with a military background or proficiency in Carpenter's Tools make it at DC 12 instead.
>
> Thane monologuing about "forty metres of prime hemp rope, Mistran-braided" while a party member crouches over rat prints is an excellent tonal beat. Play it straight.
