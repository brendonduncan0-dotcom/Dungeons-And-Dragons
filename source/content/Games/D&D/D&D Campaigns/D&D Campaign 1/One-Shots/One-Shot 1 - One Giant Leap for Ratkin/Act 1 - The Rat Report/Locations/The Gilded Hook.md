# The Gilded Hook

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The Gilded Hook occupies the ground floor of a three-storey building wedged between a tanner and a cartwright. Inside: the smell of boot leather and old stew, a noticeboard thick with pinned contracts, and a half-dozen adventurers in various states of preparation. Behind the contract desk, a half-elf woman is already looking at you with the expression of someone who has been interrupted eleven times this morning.

## Notable Features

- **Contract noticeboard:** Covered in job postings of varying urgency and legibility. The Thane job is pinned near the top, marked *standard priority — no combat expected.*
- **Side meeting room:** A cramped space with a table, three chairs, and a window overlooking the alley. A stout, overdressed merchant has been sitting in here for an hour.
- **The bar:** The Gilded Hook technically serves food and drink. The cook is indifferent and the ale is reliable.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 14 — Perception (crouching, examining the contract desk):** A Moonrat symbol is scratched into the underside of the desk's front panel — the same script found at Thane's warehouse. [[Mira Copperwick]] doesn't know it's there. The route once ran directly beneath the Gilded Hook.

## Battle Map

**Shape:** Rectangle
**Dimensions:** 50 ft. × 40 ft. (10 × 8 squares)

**Cover & Terrain:**
- Contract desk — along the North wall, centre — Half cover for anyone crouching behind it
- Bar counter — along the South wall, full width — Half cover
- Scattered tables and chairs — centre of room — Difficult terrain (can be shoved aside as a bonus action)
- Side meeting room doorway — East wall, North corner — chokepoint, single-file entry

**Interactive Objects:**
- Noticeboard — North wall, East of desk — can be torn down to scatter papers and create a distraction (no mechanical effect, good for flavour)
- Bar stools — along South wall — can be thrown as improvised weapons (1d4 bludgeoning, 20 ft. range)
- Side meeting room door — East wall — can be barred from inside (DC 14 Strength to break down)
