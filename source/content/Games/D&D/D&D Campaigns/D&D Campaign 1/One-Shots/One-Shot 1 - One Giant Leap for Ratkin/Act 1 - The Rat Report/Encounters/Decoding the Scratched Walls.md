# Decoding the Scratched Walls

**Act:** 1–2 (bridges the transition)
**Location:** [[The Scratched Wall District]]
**Encounter type:** Exploration / Skill Challenge

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Once you know to look, you see them everywhere — scratched low on walls, pressed into mortar between bricks, etched into the bases of market stalls. Always near ground level. Always in the same deliberate script. Some look ancient. Some look fresh. They're everywhere in this district, and they form a pattern.

## Setup

The symbol trail spans six blocks of the merchant quarter. The party must gather enough information to follow it to the warren entrance beneath the Coppergate Mill.

**Skill Challenge: 4 successes before 3 failures.**

- **Success:** The party follows the trail cleanly to the entrance and has time to prepare before descending.
- **Failure (3 failures reached):** The party eventually finds the entrance but makes enough noise that the warren is alerted. Guards inside have advantage on their first Perception check during [[Into the Warren]].

## Skill Challenge Checks

| Skill | DC | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Investigation | 12 | Recognise the symbols encode direction — a chevron motif always points toward the next cluster |
| Investigation | 15 | Spot that the trail doubled back twice, rerouted around a building constructed after the original path was laid |
| Nature | 12 | Fresh paw prints in the gutter follow the same route the symbols describe |
| History | 14 | The oldest symbols use a noticeably different stroke style — at least three or four distinct "generations" of authors |
| Perception | 11 | A rat on a nearby rooftop is watching the party and retreats when noticed |
| Persuasion | 13 | Talk [[Sergeant Wenda Brix]] into sharing the city guard's full map of symbol sightings (counts as 2 successes) |
| Arcana | 13 | Some symbols glow faintly under Detect Magic — a preservation enchantment keeps them from weathering |
| Thieves' Tools | 13 | Analysing the entry holes found throughout the district links them all to the same set of authors |

## Objectives

Find the warren entrance: a drainage tunnel beneath the Coppergate Mill, recently widened to roughly dinner-plate diameter. Large races (Medium or bigger) must succeed on a DC 10 Athletics check or spend 5 minutes widening it further to squeeze through.

> [!warning] Scaling
> If the party hits 2 failures and is struggling, have [[Sergeant Wenda Brix]] appear unprompted and offer her map — she's been watching them and decides to trust them. Reset the failure count to 1.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The moment a player realises "these are breadcrumbs left by intelligent rats" is a great table moment — pause on it, let it land, then confirm it with the Nature check showing fresh paw prints alongside decades-old symbols. Play up the absurdity: the symbols are right there at ankle height, all over a busy market district, and nobody has ever looked down.
