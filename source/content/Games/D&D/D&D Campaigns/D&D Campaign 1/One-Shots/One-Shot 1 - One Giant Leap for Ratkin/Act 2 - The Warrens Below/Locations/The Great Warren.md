# The Great Warren

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Beyond the drainage tunnel, the earth opens into something that has no right to be under a city street. The ceiling sits three feet high in most places. Salvaged timber shores up the walls. Bottle-glass lanterns cast amber light across sleeping nests, food stores, and a network of burrow-tubes running in every direction. On every surface, scratched in careful lines — text. Generations of it.

## Notable Features

- **The outer chambers:** Sleeping and eating areas. Dozens of ordinary rats scatter immediately at the party's entrance.
- **The Great Archive:** A larger chamber where every surface is covered in cramped but legible Common scratch-script. Notable sections: *"On the Nature of the Cheese Moon (First Principles)"*, *"The Great Lunar Survey of Nibblefang XII"*, *"Ballista Construction Notes — Generations 23 through 41"*, and *"The Sacred Trajectory Calculations."*
- **The construction chamber:** Where the ballista was assembled, then disassembled for transport to the roof. Empty now except for sawdust and copper wire scraps.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 11 — Investigation (archive walls):** An etched calendar marks every lunar eclipse for the past sixty years, with notes on what was accomplished during each one.
- **DC 13 — History (construction notes):** The ballista design evolved across at least eight eclipse cycles over four decades — each generation inherited and improved the last iteration's work.
- **DC 14 — Investigation (archive entrance):** A small glyph near the archive doorway is a persistent alarm spell. DM's discretion whether it already fired when the party entered.
- **DC 12 — Perception (archive, far wall):** A section of wall is slightly different in texture — a sealed niche. Contains the Moonrat Treasure Hoard (see [[Into the Warren]]).

## Battle Map

**Shape:** Irregular (two connected chambers with a narrow corridor between them)
**Dimensions:** Outer chamber 30 ft. × 25 ft. (6 × 5 squares); corridor 10 ft. × 10 ft. (2 × 2 squares); Archive chamber 35 ft. × 25 ft. (7 × 5 squares)

**Cover & Terrain:**
- Timber support columns — outer chamber, evenly spaced in a 2 × 2 grid — Half cover; cannot be moved
- Burrow holes — throughout all walls at ground level, every 10 ft. — impassable to Medium or larger creatures; Small creatures enter/exit as a bonus action
- Sleeping nest mounds — outer chamber, South side — Difficult terrain
- Archive wall carvings — Archive chamber, all walls — no mechanical effect; dense text provides flavour interaction
- Bottle-glass lanterns — hung at head height every 15 ft. — AC 10, 1 HP; shattering one plunges a 10 ft. radius into darkness

**Interactive Objects:**
- Timber support columns — outer chamber — can be used as half cover; cannot be toppled (load-bearing)
- Bottle-glass lanterns — throughout — can be smashed (bonus action if adjacent) to create darkness in a 10 ft. radius
- Sealed niche (archive, West wall) — DC 12 Perception to locate; contains the Moonrat Treasure Hoard

> [!tip] DM Note
> The ceiling is 3 ft. high throughout — Medium creatures are effectively crouching the entire time, which halves movement speed unless the DM handwaves it for playability. Consider calling it out once for atmosphere and then ignoring the movement penalty so combat doesn't grind. The cramped ceiling is a vibe, not a mechanic.
>
> Give the party time to read the archive walls between combat phases. The Cheese Moon Doctrine is absurd and oddly moving — generations of rats, each with only a few hours of true intelligence across their entire lives, dedicating every one of those hours to this one goal. A party that reads feels the weight of it going into Act 3.
