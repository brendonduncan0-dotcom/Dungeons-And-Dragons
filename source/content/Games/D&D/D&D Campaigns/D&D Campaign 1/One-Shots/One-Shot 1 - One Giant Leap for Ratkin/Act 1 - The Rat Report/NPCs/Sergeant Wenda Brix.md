# Sergeant Wenda Brix

**Role:** Potential ally / City guard sergeant
**Location:** [[The Scratched Wall District]]

## Personality
- Pragmatic: doesn't care who solves the problem, just that it gets solved
- Mildly contemptuous of guild adventurers on principle, but warms up quickly if they prove useful
- Quietly convinced something strange is going on; has been saying this to her superiors for weeks with no traction

## Appearance
A broad-shouldered human woman in her late thirties, city guard tabard slightly too large for her, with a notepad tucked under one arm and the confident posture of someone who has broken up a lot of fights.

## Motivation
She wants the graffiti explained and the neighbourhood quiet. If the party is making progress where she couldn't, she'll share her resources — the city guard's symbol map and access to drainage system records — in exchange for being kept in the loop.

## Secret
She found a fresh set of Moonrat symbols inside the guard barracks' cellar two weeks ago. She cleaned them off before anyone else saw them. She hasn't told anyone because she doesn't know what to do with it — if she reports it, someone will ask how rats got past the guard post, and she doesn't have a good answer.
