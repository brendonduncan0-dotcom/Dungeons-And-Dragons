# The Guild Contract

**Act:** 1
**Location:** [[The Gilded Hook]]
**Encounter type:** Social

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The Gilded Hook smells of boot leather and last night's stew. Mira Copperwick leans across the contract desk with her usual expression — professional patience worn very thin — and slides a sealed envelope toward you. "Aldric Thane. Merchant. Warehouse robbery, no obvious culprit, strange markings at the scene. He's waiting in the side room. Try not to let him talk for more than ten minutes or he'll never stop."

## Setup

[[Mira Copperwick]] briefs the party on the contract terms: 50 gold on completion, 20 gold upfront for expenses. [[Aldric Thane]] is waiting in the side room and has already been here for an hour.

Thane is a nervous, moderately wealthy importer who deals in building materials. He is convinced this is a coordinated criminal ring targeting him specifically. He is embarrassed to admit the culprits might be rats.

## NPC Goals

**Mira Copperwick:** Get this contract off her desk. She'll share everything she has (which isn't much) and redirect the party to Thane.

**Aldric Thane:** He wants his materials back and an explanation. He'll attempt to accompany the party to his warehouse; [[Mira Copperwick]] will talk him out of this if the party doesn't.

## Possible Outcomes

- **Full debrief:** The party leaves knowing the specific materials stolen (hemp rope, copper wire, timber planks), the warehouse location, that symbols were found at the scene, and that similar symbols have appeared elsewhere in the district.
- **Rushed debrief:** The party gets the basics and misses that Thane mentioned "symbols near every hole — same as the ones on Whitaker Street." This costs them one clue in Act 2 and increases the Decoding skill challenge DC by 1.

**Key skill checks:**
- Insight DC 11 — Thane is hiding that he initially dismissed the symbols as gang tags. On a success, the party can press him and learn that the symbols appear at *multiple different ages*, some clearly old.
- Persuasion DC 12 — Convince Thane to add a 10 gp personal bonus on top of the guild's standard prepayment.
