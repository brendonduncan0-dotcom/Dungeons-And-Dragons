# Aldric Thane

**Role:** Quest client / Merchant
**Location:** [[Thane's Import House]]

## Personality
- Pompous in proportion to how embarrassed he is — bluster is his default defensive move
- Detail-obsessed: will tell you the exact weight of missing rope if you let him
- Genuinely anxious underneath the bluster; this isn't the first strange thing he's noticed and it worries him

## Appearance
A stout man in his mid-forties, overdressed for a warehouse visit, with a copper merchant's seal on his signet ring and sawdust on his boots anyway.

## Motivation
Recover his materials (he won't) and get an explanation (he will). He wants to maintain the fiction that this was "a sophisticated criminal operation" and not, in any meaningful sense, rats.

## Secret
Three months ago, Thane's nightwatchman quit after claiming he saw rats "walking upright and carrying a map" near the warehouse. Thane paid him severance and told no one. He'll share this if the party makes him feel like it won't be used against him (Persuasion DC 12, or automatically if the party has already confirmed rats are involved).
