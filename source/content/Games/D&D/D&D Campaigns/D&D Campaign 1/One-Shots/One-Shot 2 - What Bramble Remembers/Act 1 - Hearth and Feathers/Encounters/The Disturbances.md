# The Disturbances

**Act:** 1 — [[Act 1 - Hearth and Feathers]]
**Location:** [[Millhaven Village]]
**Encounter type:** Exploration / Skill Challenge

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The village elder points toward the tree line without quite looking at it. "It started three weeks ago," she says. "Small things. Then the goat. Last night I heard something like an owl, but too low, too slow. Comes from that direction." She pauses. "There's a wizard out there. He's been out there for fifteen years and never caused any trouble." Another pause. "Much trouble."

## Setup

The magical disturbances radiate from Durgin's failed containment circle in his workroom. The skill challenge ends when the party reaches Durgin's door — the disturbances are formally "resolved" during [[Dinner at Durgins]] when the party helps reseal the circle.

## Objectives

Identify the source of the disturbances and assess whether it is dangerous.

## Skill Challenge

**3 successes before 2 failures.** Each success earns information; failures introduce minor complications.

| Check | DC | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arcana | 11 | The magic reads as containment-school spillover — structured, passive, not wild | Reading is muddy; something secondary is masking the source |
| Nature | 10 | Affected animals are physically unharmed; the magic is passive and non-predatory | A chicken startles and must be caught before Aldra notices (comic interlude, costs 1 minute) |
| Investigation | 12 | Disturbance gradient points clearly north-east toward the cottage | One party member steps directly under the levitating goat and is briefly elevated (comic; no damage) |
| Perception | 11 | The low owl-sound is directional — comes from inside the cottage, not from the wood | Nothing of note; no information gained |

**On 3 successes:** Party arrives at Durgin's door with a working theory — structured, probably unintentional, probably fixable.
**On 2 failures:** Party arrives uncertain; one disturbance (the goat, the weather vane) persists through dinner. Durgin is visibly relieved when they don't specifically mention the circle notation — DC 13 Insight to catch this.

## Possible Outcomes

- **Standard:** Party identifies the source with confidence and arrives prepared to be helpful
- **With failures:** Party arrives with suspicion they can't quite justify; Durgin's warmth reads as slightly too easy if they're looking for it

> [!warning] Scaling
> This is a purely investigative encounter with no combat risk. If the party rushes through it, have Bramble erupt from behind Durgin's legs the moment the door opens — the energy reset is immediate and effective.
