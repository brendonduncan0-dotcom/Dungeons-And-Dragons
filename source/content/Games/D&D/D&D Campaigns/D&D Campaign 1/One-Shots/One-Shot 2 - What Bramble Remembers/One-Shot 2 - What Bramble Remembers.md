# One-Shot 2 - What Bramble Remembers

## Premise

The party is contracted to investigate magical disturbances near Millhaven, a quiet farming village on the outskirts of Waterdeep. The source is Durgin Stonehew — a warmhearted dwarf wizard whose experimental research is leaking into the surrounding area. When the party meets him and his baby owlbear familiar, Bramble, everything seems manageable. It isn't. Durgin has been hiding something that will outlast him.

## Hook

The guild board carries a contract marked URGENT: *"Millhaven residents report unexplained magical phenomena — levitating animals, reversed compasses, unexplained sounds after dark. Locals believe the old wizard on the village edge is responsible. Assess and resolve. Client: Aldra, village elder. Reward: 80gp upon resolution (40gp upfront)."*

**Alternative hooks:**
- Rindle recognises Millhaven as the source of a rare alchemical herb he's been sourcing — his supplier wrote to him directly because the disturbances are disrupting the harvest.
- Lady Cordelia's family steward flagged an unusual complaint forwarded through the Ashworth estate's old patronage network — someone in Millhaven is making noise about a wizard and it's becoming embarrassing.
- Brok's caravan guard instincts were triggered by a merchant who passed through the guild that morning, spooked by something large he heard moving in the Thornwood at night.

## Act 1 — Hearth and Feathers

The party arrives in Millhaven to find a village quietly losing its mind: a goat hovering six inches above the ground, a weather vane spinning backwards in still air, a cat staring at nothing and hissing. Aldra, the village elder, briefs them and pays the first half of the contract. Following the disturbances leads directly to Durgin's cottage at the village edge — a warm, cluttered place that smells of pine resin and old books.

Durgin meets them at the door covered in chalk dust, immediately apologetic and cheerfully unfazed. He introduces Bramble, his eight-month-old baby owlbear, who barrels past Durgin's legs to investigate the party. The disturbances are a minor problem — a containment circle failed in his workroom. With the party's help, he seals it. He insists they stay for dinner.

Players who search the study during the meal may find a half-burned letter from Magistra Velthrane of the Arcane Academium.

**Key scenes:** [[The Disturbances]], [[Dinner at Durgins]]

## Act 2 — The Weight of the Work

Overnight, the disturbances change: colder, quieter, wrong in a way that is hard to name. Tracks at the cottage's edge lead into the Thornwood. Following them reveals what Durgin has been practicing on — small animals with corrupted spiritual signatures, flickering between states. Unsettling, but not grotesque.

The cottage is empty when the party returns. Bramble is gone. The far wall has been torn outward from the inside. Durgin's journal lies open on the workbench. Bramble's favourite toy — a chewed wooden owl — sits beside it.

The journal tells the whole story: Bramble has Greyveil Wasting, a corruption of his innate owlbear magic that will kill him within weeks. Durgin spent months researching animus transference — a form of outlawed necromancy that moves an animating soul from one vessel to another. The Arcane Academium revoked his license when they found out, cutting off access to materials and components. Durgin's final journal entry is dated the previous night.

Rindle can read Durgin's research notes without an Arcana check. He will be the one who has to explain what they mean to the rest of the party. Cordelia finds her family name in the journal — the Ashworths donated to the Academium three generations ago, granting historical "first claim" rights over research conducted under their patronage.

**Key scenes:** [[Failed Experiments]], [[The Empty Cottage]]

## Act 3 — What Bramble Remembers

Heavy tracks lead deep into the Thornwood. The party follows them by torchlight until the trees thin into a clearing. A low sound reaches them first — somewhere between a hoot and a moan, too low for a bird, too structured for wind.

A large shape steps into the torchlight. The undead owlbear is frost-rimed, pale grey eyes glowing faintly. Larger than Bramble. Wrong in a way that is immediately apparent. It stops when it sees the party.

*"...you came back."*

Durgin's voice. From somewhere inside the creature.

*"I had to see if you would."*

Magistra Velthrane arrives with two Academium guards shortly after, having tracked the ritual's magical signature. She presents legal documentation and claims the creature as Academium property — the first confirmed successful animus transference in forty years. She is not immediately violent; she believes she has the authority. Cordelia is uniquely positioned to challenge this.

The resolution belongs to the players.

**Climax:** [[What Bramble Remembers]]

## Possible Endings

- **Mercy:** Durgin asks for rest. With the party's help (DC 12 Arcana — Durgin guides them through it), a release ritual frees both souls. The owlbear's body goes still. Two souls pass on together. Velthrane leaves with documentation but no specimen.
- **Reversal:** The party attempts to separate the souls (DC 17 Arcana, group check, three successes before two failures). *Success:* Durgin's soul passes on peacefully — a proper goodbye. The undead collapses. *Failure:* Durgin's voice fades mid-sentence. The owlbear stands still, then walks into the dark. Only animal instinct remains.
- **Defiance:** The party fights off Velthrane's guards. Durgin-Bramble chooses the Thornwood. *"I'd like to see a bit more of the world. Bramble always wanted to."* The contract goes unclosed.

## DM Notes

**Pacing:** Act 1 should feel warm and unhurried. Resist the urge to signal dread early — let the players genuinely enjoy Durgin and Bramble. The levity beats are not decoration; they are load-bearing. The harder the fall, the better the story.

**The reveal:** When Durgin-Bramble speaks for the first time, pause. Don't rush to the next beat. Give the table a moment. If players immediately try to help or fight, let them — but Durgin will ask them to slow down first.

**Velthrane's arrival:** She should arrive about 5–10 minutes (real time) after the party first speaks with Durgin-Bramble. Don't let her arrival cut the emotional moment short — she can wait at the tree line.

**If players attack Durgin-Bramble:** The undead owlbear defends itself but does not pursue. Durgin's voice becomes distressed: *"Please — I don't want to hurt you. I just wanted to see you again."* This should stop most parties.

**The Cordelia hook:** Velthrane will recognise the Ashworth name immediately. Her composure will crack, briefly. The Ashworth donation predates the current Academium charter — it is a genuine legal complication she did not anticipate.

**Contract status:** The contract (resolve the disturbances) was technically completed at the end of Act 1. Aldra pays the second half regardless of Act 3 outcomes. The guild closure note should read: *"Disturbances resolved. Cause: experimental necromantic research, now concluded. Researcher deceased."*
