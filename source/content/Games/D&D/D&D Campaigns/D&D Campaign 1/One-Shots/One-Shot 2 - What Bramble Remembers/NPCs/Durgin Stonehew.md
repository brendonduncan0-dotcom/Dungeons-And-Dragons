# Durgin Stonehew

**Role:** Key NPC / Tragic figure
**First appears:** Act 1
**Location:** [[Durgin's Cottage]]

## Personality
- Warm and immediately hospitable — he offers tea before he offers his name
- Fussy about his research notes and his workshop, relaxed about everything else
- Prone to trailing off mid-sentence when a thought catches him; picks it back up three minutes later without acknowledging the gap

## Appearance
A stout dwarf in his mid-hundreds, chalk-dusted at all times, with a short silver beard knotted with a worn copper bead that Bramble chewed on as a kit.

## Motivation
Durgin has known for weeks that Bramble is dying. Every other avenue closed — licenses revoked, materials unavailable, conventional magic inadequate. Animus transference was the only thing left that could work. He chose to give himself because it was his to give.

## Secret
Durgin completed the transfer ritual the night before the party arrived in Millhaven. His body is already dead in the workroom by the time the party reaches Act 3. By the time the party meets him in Act 1, he has perhaps eighteen hours of lucidity remaining as the two souls settle into each other — but they won't know this until Act 3. He does not tell them.

## Bramble

Bramble is an eight-month-old owlbear — small enough to fit under a table, old enough to have opinions. His feathers are cream-brown, his beak is pale, and he has enormous round amber eyes that he deploys strategically when he wants food. He treats Durgin as a climbing frame and the party immediately as potential friends.

**Bramble's behaviours in Act 1:**
- Steals something from Rindle's pack (Rindle may oppose with a contested Sleight of Hand — Bramble has +4, and he has already committed before anyone notices)
- Follows Draco in exact lockstep when Draco moves across the room, convinced this is a game
- Knocks over Cordelia's chair by walking under it with insufficient spatial awareness; sits on her foot as an apology
- Brings Durgin a boot when chalk dust irritates Durgin's eyes — this is apparently a comfort gesture; Durgin accepts it and sets it carefully to one side

Bramble's favourite toy is a small carved wooden owl, worn smooth by months of chewing. It is left on the workbench beside Durgin's open journal in Act 2.
