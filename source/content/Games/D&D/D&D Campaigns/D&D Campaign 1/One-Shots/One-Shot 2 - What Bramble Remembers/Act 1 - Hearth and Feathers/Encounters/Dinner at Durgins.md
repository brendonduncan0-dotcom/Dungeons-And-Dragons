# Dinner at Durgins

**Act:** 1 — [[Act 1 - Hearth and Feathers]]
**Location:** [[Durgin's Cottage]]
**Encounter type:** Social / Light Challenge

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The dwarf is already talking before the door is fully open. "Yes, yes, the disturbances — terribly sorry, a circle went sideways, I've been meaning to reseal it, come in, you look like you haven't eaten since Waterdeep." He steps aside. From behind his legs, something the size of a large dog barrels past him, stops, and stares at you with enormous amber eyes. It makes a sound like a satisfied barn owl. It has feathers. It has a beak. It has already identified Rindle's pack as a point of interest.

## Setup

The party's introduction to Durgin and Bramble. The meal is real, the hospitality is genuine, and Durgin is exactly what he appears to be — a lonely wizard who enjoys company. This encounter has two beats: the social dinner, and a minor challenge when the containment circle destabilises mid-meal.

## NPC Goals

**Durgin:** Wants the party to enjoy the food, feel welcome, and not look too closely at the workroom. He deflects questions about the Academium letter if found — "old bureaucratic business, nothing interesting." He is genuinely glad they're here.

**Bramble:** Wants food, attention, and Brok specifically, in that order.

## Bramble Beats

Run as many as fit the table's energy:

- **The heist:** Bramble steals something from Rindle's pack. Rindle may oppose with a contested Sleight of Hand (Bramble +4). Bramble has already committed before anyone notices the attempt is in progress.
- **The shadow:** Bramble follows Brok in exact lockstep when Brok crosses the room. If Brok stops, Bramble stops. If Brok turns around, Bramble sits down and looks at the ceiling.
- **The chair incident:** Bramble walks under Cordelia's chair with insufficient spatial awareness and tips it. He immediately sits on her foot. This is his apology. He means it.
- **The boot:** When chalk dust irritates Durgin's eyes mid-conversation, Bramble disappears and returns with a boot from the bedroom. Durgin takes it gravely and sets it to one side. *"He does this. I don't know what it means either."*

## The Circle Problem

Halfway through dinner, the workroom hums. Durgin sighs and sets down his fork. *"That would be the circle. Would anyone mind? I need two anchor points while I redraw the centre inscription — it's quick."*

**Mechanics:**
- Durgin handles the inscription (no check required)
- Two party members each make DC 12 Arcana to hold anchor points; one other member may Help to grant advantage
- **Success:** Circle sealed; disturbances end; Durgin quietly, genuinely grateful
- **Failure (one missed check):** Minor discharge — a jar shatters, one party member's hair stands on end for the rest of the evening, a faint smell of burnt copper. Circle is still sealed. Durgin apologises for the hair.

## NPC Goals (Durgin deflection)

If asked about the workroom notation:
*"Old notation, nothing current — research from some years back. I do keep meaning to clean the floor. More vegetables?"*

**Insight DC 13:** He changed the subject very smoothly. He has done this before.

## Possible Outcomes

- **Warm exit:** Party leaves genuinely charmed by Durgin and Bramble, no suspicion — they'll be harder hit by Act 2
- **Curious exit:** Party found the letter (DC 14) or caught Durgin's deflection (Insight DC 13) — they have a thread but don't know what it connects to
- **Suspicious exit:** Party is specifically suspicious of Durgin but can't articulate why — useful if a player wants to stay on guard

> [!warning] Scaling
> The circle problem is optional. If the social roleplay has fully captured the table, skip it or run it as flavour only — the circle pops on its own with a smell of burnt copper, Durgin winces, and dinner continues. The purpose of this scene is to make the party care about these two. Everything else is secondary.
