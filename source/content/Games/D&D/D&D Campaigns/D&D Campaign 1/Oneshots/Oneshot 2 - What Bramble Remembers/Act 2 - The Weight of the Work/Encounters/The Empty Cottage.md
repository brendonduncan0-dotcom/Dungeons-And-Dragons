# The Empty Cottage

**Act:** 2 — [[Act 2 - The Weight of the Work]]
**Location:** [[Durgin's Cottage]]
**Encounter type:** Exploration / Roleplay

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The cottage door is ajar. The fire is out. When you push it open, the first thing you notice is the cold — and then the hole in the far wall of the workroom, jagged and outward-facing, large enough for something substantial to have passed through. The chalk circle on the floor is burned black at the centre. Durgin's workbench is clear except for two things: an open journal, and a small carved wooden owl, worn smooth and slightly damp, as if someone held it very recently.

## Setup

This is the emotional turning point of the adventure. There is no enemy here, no skill check required to understand what happened. The party finds Durgin's body and reads his journal. This scene should run slowly.

## What They Find

**Durgin's body** is seated at the north end of the room, inside the ritual circle, hands folded in his lap. He looks peaceful. A DC 11 Medicine check confirms he died within the past few hours — cardiac arrest consistent with extreme magical exertion. There are no marks on him.

**Durgin's journal** lies open to the final entry. Players who page back through it find the full history. Rindle can read the research notes without a check. Other characters require DC 14 Arcana.

## The Journal

*Present each section as the party reads backward through the journal, or summarise for non-readers.*

> [!info] Journal — Two Months Ago
> Notes on Greyveil Wasting: a corruption of innate magical energy that manifests in creatures with arcane nature. No known cure. Progression: loss of coordination, loss of appetite, weakening of the innate magic that sustains the creature's hybrid form. Terminal within three to four weeks of onset. Bramble is in the second week.
>
> Correspondence with the Arcane Academium requesting a research waiver to pursue animus transference. Response: denied, pending review.

> [!info] Journal — Six Weeks Ago
> Practice experiments on small animals in the Thornwood hollow. Partial results only — the transfer requires a complete soul as the animating source; a fragment or partial signature won't sustain a failing vessel. Three subjects in partial state, stable but unresolved. Notes this may be permanent.
>
> Academium revocation notice received via courier. All materials and components seized. Research license suspended. Durgin's note on this: *"Well. That closes that."*

> [!info] Journal — Final Entry (last night)
> *"Bramble ate his dinner tonight. The wooden owl is on the bench — I didn't tell him why I left it there. I think he knows, though. He's smarter than people give him credit for. Most people are."*
>
> *"The transfer requires a complete soul. I have one. It's mine to give. The Academium will call this reckless. They'll be right. I've been reckless about exactly one thing in a hundred and forty years, and it's this ridiculous, wonderful, impossible animal, and I find I don't mind."*
>
> *"If it works — and I think it will work — I'll be in there somewhere. I expect Bramble will be in there too. I don't know what that will feel like. I expect it will feel like him. I'm all right with that."*
>
> *"If the guild adventurers come back looking for me: they were very good company. Tell them I'm sorry about the contract fee."*

## PC Hooks

**Rindle:** He reads the research notes and understands what they mean before he finishes the first page. He will be the one who has to explain the mechanics to the rest of the party. This is not a comfortable thing to have to do.

**Lady Cordelia:** The Ashworth name appears mid-journal — the Ashworths donated to the Academium three generations ago, granting historical first-claim rights over research conducted under their patronage. Cordelia may recognise the legal implication or simply feel a complicated guilt-by-association. Either is valid.

## Possible Outcomes

- Party reads the journal fully and understands what happened; they follow the tracks
- A party member may need a moment before they're ready to move. Allow it.

> [!tip] DM Note
> If players try to help Durgin here — Spare the Dying, Healing Word, anything at all — let them try. None of it works. He's been gone for hours. Then give them the journal.
>
> The wooden owl should be mentioned specifically when describing the workbench. Players who remember Bramble carrying it during Act 1 will understand what its presence on the bench means.
