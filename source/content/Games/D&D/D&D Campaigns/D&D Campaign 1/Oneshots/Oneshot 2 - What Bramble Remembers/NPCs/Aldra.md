**Role:** Quest giver / Village elder
**First appears:** Act 1
**Location:** [[Millhaven Village]]

## Personality
- Practical and straightforward — she describes problems clearly and doesn't embellish
- Privately fond of Durgin despite her exasperation; she's complained about him to every passing merchant for years but would never actually report him to the city
- Slightly guilty about filing the guild contract; she hoped the party would fix whatever Durgin broke and leave quietly

## Appearance
A stout woman in her sixties with calloused hands and a grey braid pinned under a wide-brimmed hat she wears in all weather.

## Motivation
Aldra wants the disturbances to stop before the village loses another animal to the hovering problem. She does not want Durgin punished or removed — she wants him to be more careful.

## Secret
Aldra knew Durgin was sick with grief about Bramble. A month ago she brought him a pie and found him crying over a pile of research notes. She didn't ask what they said. She wishes she had.
