> [!info] Read Aloud
> The trees here are old and close together — not a managed wood but a place that has been left entirely to its own devices. The undergrowth is dense at the edges and thins toward the interior, where the canopy blocks enough light that nothing grows from the floor. It is quiet in the specific way places get when animals have decided to be somewhere else.

## Notable Features

- Tracks in the soft ground near the cottage's torn wall — initially large but irregular, as if whatever made them was finding its footing
- A cleared hollow 200 ft. north of the cottage where bark has been stripped from several trees at roughly six feet of height, in parallel lines
- A cold spot approximately halfway into the wood — temperature drops noticeably without wind or shade to explain it
- A faint blue-grey light visible deeper in, when viewed from the hollow at night

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 — Survival:** The tracks belong to something significantly larger than Bramble — four-legged, heavy, with a gait pattern unlike a normal owlbear. Or something that was recently much smaller.
- **DC 14 — Investigation (stripped bark):** The claw marks follow a repeated pattern, not random damage. Something was testing its reach and strength. Methodically.
- **DC 15 — Arcana (cold spot):** Residual necrotic energy, recent. Not dangerous at this concentration, but significant — whatever happened nearby involved soul-magic of considerable complexity, completed within the past twelve hours.

> [!tip] DM Note
> The Thornwood is a pressure ramp — it should feel like walking toward something the party isn't sure they want to find. Describe the cold spot and the stripped bark as separate discoveries at separate moments. The blue-grey light visible from the hollow is the Binding Glade. Let players see it and choose to move toward it. That choice is important.
