# Failed Experiments

**Act:** 2 — [[Act 2 - The Weight of the Work]]
**Location:** [[The Thornwood]]
**Encounter type:** Exploration

> [!info] Read Aloud
> In a hollow between two fallen trunks, you find them: a rabbit, a fox, a crow. All three are alive. All three are looking at you. The crow is perched on the fox. The rabbit is sitting inside the curve of the fox's forepaw. Neither the fox nor the rabbit reacts to this. When you come closer, all three turn their heads to look at you at the same time, at the same speed, in the same direction.

## Setup

These are Durgin's early failed experiments — attempts to practice animus transference on small animals before committing to the full procedure. The failures didn't kill the subjects; they produced a partial state where the transfer didn't complete, leaving multiple creatures sharing a single set of responses. They are not in pain. They are not dangerous. They are very wrong.

## Objectives

Understand what the party is looking at. No combat required or recommended.

## Investigation

| Check | DC | Information |
|---|---|---|
| Arcana | 13 | Residual soul-magic in and around the animals — a transfer was attempted but didn't fully complete. Two or more spiritual signatures occupying one body, or multiple bodies responding as one distributed awareness. |
| Medicine | 12 | Physically healthy. Whatever is happening to them is metaphysical, not physiological. Their bodies are fine. |
| Nature | 11 | These animals would not normally be near each other. Something drew them here, or they stayed after the procedure rather than dispersing. |
| Insight | 14 | The animals are waiting for something. They are not afraid. They are also not entirely present — as though part of their awareness is elsewhere. |

## Possible Outcomes

- Party understands they are looking at failed soul-magic experiments before they reach the cottage
- Party does not understand what they are looking at until they read the journal; seeing this scene again in memory after the journal will hit differently

> [!tip] DM Note
> Do not overplay this scene. It should be quietly disturbing, not horrifying. If players try to help the animals, they can't — no spell available to level 3 characters addresses this. A DC 15 Arcana check tells them the state may resolve on its own over time, or may not. Let the party sit with that and move on when ready.
>
> If players harmed the animals in the hollow — Durgin will know when the party meets him in Act 3. He'll mention it. Not angrily. Just: *"The animals in the hollow. I know what you found. I hoped they'd disperse."* This is a minor character beat, not a mechanical consequence. But it will land.
