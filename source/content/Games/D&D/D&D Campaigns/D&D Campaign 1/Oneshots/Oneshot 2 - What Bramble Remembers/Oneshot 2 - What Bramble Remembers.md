## Premise

The party is contracted to investigate magical disturbances near Millhaven, a quiet farming village on the outskirts of Waterdeep. The source is Durgin Stonehew — a warmhearted dwarf wizard whose experimental research is leaking into the surrounding area. When the party meets him and his baby owlbear familiar, Bramble, everything seems manageable. It isn't.

## Themes

- Grief and sacrifice — a parent figure choosing to give everything for someone they love
- Found family — the party is asked to care about two people they've known for one evening
- Institutional authority vs. individual conscience — the Academium was legally correct and morally wrong
- Loss and letting go — all three endings involve losing something; none of them are clean
- Light body horror (mild) — the merged form is unsettling, not grotesque
- Emotional roleplaying — the climax is a conversation, not a fight

## Hooks

The guild board carries a contract marked URGENT: *"Millhaven residents report unexplained magical phenomena — levitating animals, reversed compasses, unexplained sounds after dark. Locals believe the old wizard on the village edge is responsible. Assess and resolve. Client: Aldra, village elder. Reward: 80gp upon resolution (40gp upfront)."*

**Alternative hooks:**
- Rindle recognises Millhaven as the source of a rare alchemical herb he's been sourcing — his supplier wrote to him directly because the disturbances are disrupting the harvest.
- Lady Cordelia's family steward flagged an unusual complaint forwarded through the Ashworth estate's old patronage network.

## Possible Endings

- **Mercy:** Durgin asks for rest. With the party's help (DC 12 Arcana — Durgin guides them), a release ritual frees both souls. The owlbear's body goes still. Velthrane leaves with documentation but no specimen.
- **Reversal:** The party attempts to separate the souls (DC 17 Arcana group check, three successes before two failures). *Success:* Durgin passes on peacefully — a proper goodbye. *Failure:* His voice fades mid-sentence; the owlbear walks into the dark with only animal instinct remaining.
- **Defiance:** The party fights off Velthrane's guards and lets Durgin-Bramble choose. *"I'd like to see a bit more of the world. Bramble always wanted to."* The contract goes unclosed.

## DM Notes

**Emotional shape:** This session should feel warm in Act 1, unsettling in Act 2, and quietly devastating in Act 3. The levity beats with Bramble are not decoration — they are load-bearing. The harder the players have fallen for him and Durgin by the end of Act 1, the better everything that follows lands.

**Pacing:** Do not rush Act 1. Act 2 is atmosphere and discovery — let the Thornwood breathe. Act 3 belongs to the players; once Durgin speaks, get out of their way.

**The contract:** Technically resolved at the end of Act 1. Aldra pays the second half regardless of how Act 3 ends. Guild closure note: *"Disturbances resolved. Cause: experimental necromantic research, now concluded. Researcher deceased."*
