# Thane's Import House

> [!info] Read Aloud
> A solid two-storey warehouse near the eastern docks district. The ground floor is open storage — or was. Most of the lumber and rope stock is simply gone, leaving gaps in the racks and sawdust outlines on the floor. Three ragged holes punch through the lower wall, each roughly the size of a dinner plate, edges chewed through brick. Near each hole, small precise marks are scratched into the mortar.

## Notable Features

- **The missing stock area:** Other materials left completely untouched — only hemp rope, copper wire, and timber planks were taken, as if from a list.
- **The entry holes:** Three in the lower wall, chewed through brick. Scratch marks visible around each. A cluster of small symbols is pressed into the mortar near every hole.
- **The alley behind:** Visible through the holes — a drainage grate in the alley floor is slightly displaced, with disturbed dust around it.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 10 — Investigation:** Entry holes were made from outside; drag marks in the sawdust show material was hauled outward through them, not inward.
- **DC 13 — Investigation:** The symbols near each hole are in slightly different styles and depths, suggesting multiple authors at different times.
- **DC 14 — Nature:** Paw prints in the alley dust lead directly to the displaced drainage grate.
- **DC 15 — History:** The combination of materials taken — rope, copper wire, and timber planks in these quantities — matches a ballista construction list.

## Battle Map

**Shape:** Rectangle
**Dimensions:** 60 ft. × 40 ft. (12 × 8 squares)

**Cover & Terrain:**
- Storage racks (remaining stock) — along the North wall and centre of room — Half cover; can be toppled (DC 13 Athletics, creates 10 ft. difficult terrain on fall line)
- Entry holes — South wall, evenly spaced — too small for Medium creatures, exit points for Small creatures only
- Main loading doors — West wall, centre — double doors, currently closed

**Interactive Objects:**
- Storage racks — North wall and centre — can be toppled onto enemies (DC 13 Athletics as an action; target in adjacent square makes DC 13 Dexterity save or takes 2d6 bludgeoning and is Prone)
- Drainage grate (alley, outside South wall) — leads to [[The Scratched Wall District]] trail; requires DC 10 Athletics to lift
- Hanging pulley and rope — ceiling, centre — DC 12 Athletics to swing across the room, avoiding ground-level difficult terrain
