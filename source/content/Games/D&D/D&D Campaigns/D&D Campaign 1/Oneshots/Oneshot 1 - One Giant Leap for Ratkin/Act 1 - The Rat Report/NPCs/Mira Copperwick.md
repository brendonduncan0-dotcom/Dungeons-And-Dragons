# Mira Copperwick

**Role:** Guild clerk / Contract issuer
**Location:** [[The Gilded Hook]]

## Personality
- Efficient to the point of curtness — says what needs to be said and nothing more
- Has seen genuinely bizarre contracts before and is unfazed; her deadpan delivery makes absurd situations funnier
- Quietly proud of the guild and invested in its reputation, though she'd never admit it

## Appearance
A compact half-elf woman in her fifties with ink-stained fingers, reading glasses perpetually perched on her forehead, and the expression of someone who has been interrupted eleven times this morning.

## Motivation
Keep the guild's contract board moving and its members employed. She has enough information to be useful and the good sense to stay out of the field.

## Secret
She recognised the symbols from Thane's description immediately — she saw similar marks on the wall behind the guild's own cellar years ago and dismissed them as animal scratching. She won't mention this unless pressed directly (Persuasion DC 14 or Insight DC 12 to notice she's holding something back). If the party finds this out, the guild's cellar contains a faded but complete set of Moonrat waypoint symbols — the route once ran directly under the Gilded Hook.
