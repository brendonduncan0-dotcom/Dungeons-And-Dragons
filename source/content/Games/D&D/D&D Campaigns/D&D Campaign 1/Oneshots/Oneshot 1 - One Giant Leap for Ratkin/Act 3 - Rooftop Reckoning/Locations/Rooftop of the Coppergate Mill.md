# Rooftop of the Coppergate Mill

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The mill roof is flat and wide — the highest point in this part of the district, with a clear line of sight east across the rooftops to the open sky. Tonight, the moon is full and bright. At the centre of the roof stands a ballista assembled from rope and timber, elegant in a brutal and functional way. Three rats work around it in urgent, purposeful silence.
>
> Above, the shadow is already crossing the moon. The eclipse has begun.

## Notable Features

- **The ballista:** Assembled from Thane's stolen materials. Aimed directly at the moon, bolt loaded and ready. AC 12, HP 18. Fires in 3 rounds from the party's arrival unless stopped.
- **The rooftop edge:** A 2-foot parapet around the perimeter. Falling deals 3d6 bludgeoning damage (30-foot drop). Counts as difficult terrain when combat is near the edge.
- **The millstone access hatch:** The only entry from inside the mill. Steep stairs, small hatch — Medium creatures must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or stumble onto the roof.
- **The copper sash:** Worn across [[Grand Squeaker Nibblefang XLII]]'s chest. Fraying, repaired countless times, worn by forty-one predecessors. Worth nothing in gold; everything to the Moonrat Order.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 — Perception:** A fuse cord runs from the ballista's trigger along the North parapet to the North-East corner — a remote-fire contingency the Sappers prepared in case they needed to clear the area.
- **DC 14 — Investigation (ballista):** The aim is calculable. On a success, the character can determine exactly where the bolt will land if fired, and roughly where it would arc back down if it misses the moon.

## Battle Map

**Shape:** Rectangle with 2 ft. parapet on all sides
**Dimensions:** 50 ft. × 40 ft. (10 × 8 squares)

**Cover & Terrain:**
- Parapet wall — all four edges, 5 ft. wide — Half cover for creatures crouching behind it; standing on the parapet requires a DC 10 Acrobatics check or fall prone
- Ballista frame — centre of roof, 10 ft. × 5 ft. — Three-quarters cover for creatures sheltering behind it; AC 12, HP 18
- Access hatch — South wall, centre — difficult terrain; a creature entering from below must spend 10 ft. of movement to climb through
- Fuse cord — runs along the North parapet from the ballista to the North-East corner — can be cut (any slashing weapon, no check required) to disable the remote-fire mechanism

**Interactive Objects:**
- Ballista — centre — can be sabotaged (DC 13 Dexterity/Sleight of Hand or Tinker's Tools as an action) or destroyed (AC 12, HP 18); fires in 3 rounds if not stopped
- Fuse cord — North parapet — cutting it prevents remote firing; does not disable the manual trigger
- Parapet edge — all sides — a creature pushed to the edge makes a DC 12 Strength saving throw or falls 30 ft. to the alley below (3d6 bludgeoning)
- Access hatch — South wall — can be barred from above (an action; DC 15 Strength to force open from below)

> [!tip] DM Note
> Give the party a cinematic beat on arrival — the eclipse is happening, the moon is right there, the ballista is pointed at it. Let them take it in for a moment before Nibblefang speaks. The scale of the absurdity should land fully before the scene accelerates into chaos.
>
> The parapet edge is the most dangerous terrain feature — Moonrat Sappers will try to use Rally or thrown debris to push PCs toward it. Make sure players can see the drop.
