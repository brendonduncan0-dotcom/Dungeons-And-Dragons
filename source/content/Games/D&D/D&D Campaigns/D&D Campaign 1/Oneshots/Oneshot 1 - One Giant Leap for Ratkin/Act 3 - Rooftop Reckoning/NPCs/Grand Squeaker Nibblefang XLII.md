# Grand Squeaker Nibblefang XLII

**Role:** Villain (sympathetic) / Eclipse-form boss
**Location:** [[Rooftop of the Coppergate Mill]]

## Personality
- During eclipse: measured, eloquent, deeply philosophical — a true believer who has spent his brief intelligent life in preparation for this moment; genuinely curious about the party and willing to argue; considers dismissing their objections beneath him
- Outside eclipse: a rat — large, perhaps with slightly brighter eyes than usual, but a rat nonetheless

## Appearance
A large grey rat, notably bigger than typical, with a small sash of braided copper wire worn across his chest — the ceremonial garb of the Grand Squeaker, passed down through forty-one predecessors and repaired so many times it's more patch than original. During the eclipse, his posture changes: he stands slightly straighter, and his eyes hold a quality that doesn't belong in an animal.

## Motivation
Fulfil the sacred mission of the Moonrat Order: harvest the Great Cheese Moon. He believes utterly in this goal and will die for it. He has also thought about it very carefully and considers the party's objections intellectually weak — he welcomes the debate.

**In negotiation, Nibblefang will counter every argument:**
- *"The moon is made of rock."* → "A rock that reflects light with such perfect, luminous warmth? Absurd. That is the glow of aged gouda."
- *"A ballista bolt can't reach the moon."* → "Forty-two generations of engineers disagree. The trajectory is flawless."
- *"The bolt will just fall back down and hit someone."* → (He hasn't considered this. Insight DC 11 reveals a flicker of doubt — press it.)

## Secret
The 42 generations of Moonrat grand squeakers have each had, on average, four to six hours of total eclipse-intelligence across their entire lives. Nibblefang has spent every eclipse minute studying the archive and preparing for tonight. He knows, on some level, that if this fails, the next generation may never pick up the cause — there are fewer Moonrats now than in previous generations, and the Order is fading. This is the last real shot. He won't say this, but a character who succeeds on a DC 14 Insight check during negotiation can sense it — there is grief beneath the conviction.

## Stat Block
See [[Rooftop Reckoning]] for full eclipse-form stat block. Outside the eclipse, treat as a standard Moonrat (CR 1/8).
