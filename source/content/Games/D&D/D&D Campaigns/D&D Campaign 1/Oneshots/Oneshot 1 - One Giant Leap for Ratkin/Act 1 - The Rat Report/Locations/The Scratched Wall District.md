# The Scratched Wall District

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Six blocks of the merchant quarter, seen with new eyes. The symbols are everywhere — pressed low into every wall at ankle height, consistent and deliberate. Some are barely visible, weathered over years. Some look fresh. The district hums with market-day traffic. Nobody is looking down.

## Notable Features

- **The trail:** Symbols appear every twenty to thirty feet along a winding route through streets, alleys, and a short stretch of open plaza, always staying close to walls and avoiding open ground.
- **The age variation:** Visible to anyone who looks closely — some symbols are shallow and eroded, others crisp and dark, as if written by different hands across many years.
- **The convergence point:** Every trail in the district eventually feeds into the same alley behind the Coppergate Mill, ending at a drainage grate in the floor.

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 — Investigation:** The symbols encode direction — a recurring chevron motif always points toward the next cluster, forming a readable path.
- **DC 13 — Arcana:** Some symbols glow faintly under Detect Magic, carrying a preservation enchantment that prevents weathering. Someone has been renewing this periodically.
- **DC 14 — History:** The age variation suggests this system has been actively maintained and extended over at least thirty to forty years — far longer than any single vandal's project.

## Battle Map

> [!tip] No fixed combat encounter occurs here — this is a skill challenge location spread across several city blocks. If combat does break out (e.g. the party is ambushed by a moonrat scout), use the alley section below as the mat layout.

**Shape:** L-shaped alley
**Dimensions:** 40 ft. × 10 ft. with a 20 ft. × 10 ft. perpendicular branch (total 12 × 2 + 4 × 2 squares)

**Cover & Terrain:**
- Market stalls — North side of main alley, evenly spaced — Half cover behind stall counters
- Stacked crates — East end of main alley — Three-quarters cover stack; can be shoved (DC 11 Athletics, creates 5 ft. difficult terrain)
- Drainage grate — South end, centre — difficult terrain; can be opened as an action (DC 10 Athletics) to reveal the tunnel to [[The Great Warren]]

**Interactive Objects:**
- Market stall awnings — North wall — can be cut loose (one slash with a slashing weapon) to drop canvas and obscure a 10 ft. square (Lightly Obscured until cleared)
- Drainage grate — South end — opens to the warren tunnel; Small creatures can drop in as a bonus action, Medium creatures as an action
