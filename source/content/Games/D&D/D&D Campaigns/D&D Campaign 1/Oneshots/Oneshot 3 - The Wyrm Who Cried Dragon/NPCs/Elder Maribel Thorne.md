**Role:** Quest giver
**First appears:** Act 1
**Location:** [[Thistlewick Village Square]]

## Personality
- Practical to the point of bluntness — she's been rationing the village's grain for a "dragon tax" for two months and has no patience left for pageantry
- Quietly proud; hates that she had to send for outside help and will not admit how frightened she's been
- Protective of her people first, coin second — she'll pay the guild's fee out of her own strongbox before letting a farmer go hungry

## Appearance
Iron-grey hair scraped into a tight knot, a farmer's calloused hands, and a burn scar across one forearm she claims is from a cookfire and absolutely is not.

## Motivation
Maribel wants the "dragon" gone and her village's harvest back, full stop. She doesn't care how the guild does it — steel, silver tongue, or a clever trick — as long as Thistlewick stops bleeding grain and gold to something in the hills. She is not looking for a fight; she's looking for the tribute to stop.

## Secret
The burn scar is real, and it isn't from a cookfire. Three weeks ago she climbed partway up the tribute road at night to see the dragon for herself and got singed by a blast of fire from something she never actually saw clearly in the dark. She hasn't told anyone the size of the flame was underwhelming for what's supposedly an ancient dragon — she assumed she was just lucky to survive, and doesn't trust her own judgment on it. If a perceptive party member draws this detail out of her (DC 13 Insight or Persuasion), it's the first hard clue that "Vhalzareth" isn't what the village thinks.

## Guild Contract
Maribel offers the standard contract on behalf of Thistlewick: **75 gp per party member plus reasonable expenses**, paid from the village's own coffers, for making "the dragon problem" go away — however the party judges best. She's explicit that she'd rather the party find a way to stop the tribute without burning the village to the ground in the process, and she'll say so directly if asked what she wants beyond "gone."
