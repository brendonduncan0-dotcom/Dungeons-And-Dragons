**Role:** Minor antagonist / combat encounter leader
**First appears:** Act 1
**Location:** [[Thistlewick Outskirts]]

## Personality
- Devout to the point of absurdity — Grix has never seen "Vhalzareth" and has built an entire elaborate mental image of what his god must look like, none of it accurate
- Bossy toward the other kobolds, desperate to be the one who finally delivers a "worthy" tribute and earns recognition
- Not actually cruel — Grix wants tribute delivered, not blood spilled, and will call off the fight if given an opening

## Appearance
Wears a crown of bent nails and bottle caps he's convinced looks "draconic," and carries a hand-drawn scroll depicting his imagined vision of Vhalzareth (comically, hilariously wrong — more chicken than dragon).

## Motivation
Grix leads a small kobold band that has attached itself to the "Vhalzareth" cult, believing the ancient dragon is a genuine god worth serving. He's trying to intercept a tribute cart before it reaches the village so his band can deliver it directly to the lair themselves and be recognized as the dragon's chosen servants — a promotion, in kobold terms.

## Secret
Grix and his band have never once seen Vhalzareth. Everything they know is secondhand rumor and Grix's own embellishment. If captured and questioned (rather than killed), Grix is an easy, comedic source of exposition — he'll happily describe his god in absurd, contradictory detail, and any perceptive player will realize the kobolds are describing something that doesn't match anything the village has reported.
