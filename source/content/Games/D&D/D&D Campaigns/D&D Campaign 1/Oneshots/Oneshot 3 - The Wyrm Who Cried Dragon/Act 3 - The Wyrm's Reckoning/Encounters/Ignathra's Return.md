**Act:** 3 — [[Act 3 - The Wyrm's Reckoning]]
**Location:** [[Thistlewick Village Square]]
**Encounter type:** Social (with real combat risk if mishandled)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> A shadow eclipses the sun. Villagers scream and scatter as a vast red-scaled shape banks low over the square, wind from its wings snapping the tribute banner clean off its post. It lands atop the largest cottage, talons cracking the roof beam, and lowers its head until one enormous eye fills the space above the well. **"Where,"** it says, in a voice that makes the ground hum, **"is my son?"**

## Setup
Nix's mother, Ignathra, has been searching the region for weeks and has finally traced him to Thistlewick. She lands in the village square in full view of everyone — furious, frightened, and dangerous. How this plays out depends entirely on what the party did in Act 2:

- **If Nix was exposed and is present or accounted for:** Ignathra's opening demand can be answered directly. This is the easiest path to a peaceful resolution.
- **If Nix fled, was harmed, or his fate is unknown:** Ignathra assumes the worst, and the encounter opens much closer to violence.
- **If the party is helping Nix continue the con:** Ignathra doesn't care about the tribute scheme itself — only about Nix's safety — but she will react very badly to any sign the village or the party is using her son rather than protecting him.

## Objectives
De-escalate Ignathra by proving Nix is safe, unharmed, and not being exploited — through evidence, testimony, or producing him directly. Failing that, survive and retreat; this encounter is not meant to be won by force at this level.

## NPC Goals
Ignathra wants Nix, safe, in front of her, now. Everything else — the tribute scheme, the village's fear, the guild's involvement — is secondary and she says so if given the chance to speak rather than fight.

## Skill Challenge

| Check | DC | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persuasion or Insight (producing/describing Nix safely) | 14 | Ignathra's Frightful Presence and hostility drop immediately; she lands and listens | She remains airborne, circling, and repeats her demand more forcefully |
| Deception (only if the party is concealing something about Nix's fate) | 16 | Buys one more round of conversation before she presses further | She sees through it instantly and Frightful Presence triggers on the whole party |
| Persuasion (negotiating terms afterward — tribute, Nix's future, village safety) | 13 | Ignathra agrees to a workable arrangement (see Possible Outcomes) and departs | She leaves ambiguous, tense, and likely to return unannounced later |

## Possible Outcomes
- **Full Victory:** Nix is produced safe and unharmed (or the party convincingly demonstrates he was never in danger). Ignathra, relieved, collects her son and departs — potentially agreeing to leave Thistlewick alone entirely, con or no con, provided nobody is exploiting him going forward.
- **Partial Victory:** Ignathra is satisfied enough not to attack but remains suspicious and cold — she takes Nix and leaves with a clear warning that she'll be watching the village, and any future trouble will not be met with words.
- **Failure / Pyrrhic:** The negotiation collapses — Nix's fate is unknown, harmed, or the party is caught misrepresenting the situation. Ignathra attacks. This should be run as a genuinely dangerous, telegraphed fight with a clear retreat option (into buildings, down the well, scattering to break line of sight) rather than a guaranteed TPK — she is willing to let fleeing targets go once her point is made, since her goal was never to raze the village, only to make her fury known.

> [!warning] This is a negotiation encounter, not a boss fight
> See [[Ignathra]]'s DM Note for guidance on running this without it becoming an unwinnable slog. Combat should be a real possibility the party fears, not the expected default outcome.
