## Notable Features

- A tribute cart, half-loaded with sacks of grain and a small chest, parked and waiting by the well
- The crooked tribute banner, clearly repainted more than once as the date keeps changing
- A scorched black streak up one cottage wall, old and weathered, that nobody has bothered to repair

## Hidden Details

- **DC 12 Investigation (scorch mark):** The burn pattern is narrow and shallow — consistent with a small, fast gout of flame, not the wide devastating blast an ancient dragon's breath would leave behind.
- **DC 14 Insight (talking to villagers):** Most villagers describe the "dragon" only by rumor — voice heard from a distance, shape seen briefly at night. Nobody in the square has actually stood close to it and lived to describe it in detail, which the perceptive will note is strange for a threat supposedly two months into a regular tribute arrangement.

> [!tip] DM Note
> This is the calm, comedic-tinged opening location — play up the absurdity of a village organizing its whole calendar around "tribute day" for something almost nobody has actually seen up close. In Act 3, this same square becomes the site of Ignathra's arrival: same physical space, now filled with panicking villagers and a genuinely dangerous adult dragon overhead. Don't redecorate the square itself between visits — the tonal whiplash of the *familiar* place suddenly hosting real danger is the point.

## Image Generator Details

**Exterior:** Small farming village square, packed dirt and patchy grass ground, ringed by low timber-and-thatch cottages with smoke-stained chimneys. Open farmland and low rolling hills visible beyond the rooftops to the north.

**Layout:** Roughly circular open square with a stone well at the centre, cottages arranged around the perimeter, a single dirt road entering from the south.

**Primary Entrance:** South

**Cover & Terrain:**
- Stone well — centre of the square — waist-high circular stone lip, cracked along one side
- Tribute cart — east side of the well — wooden farm cart loaded with grain sacks and a small iron-bound chest
- Cottages — ringing the square — timber-framed, thatched roofs, several with dark, cold chimneys

**Interactive Objects:**
- Tribute cart — east of the well — can be searched, inspected for the scorch-pattern clue, or used as cover
- Crooked banner — north side, above the largest building's door — repeatedly repainted with a new tribute date

The same square now in chaos: the tribute cart overturned and grain spilled across the dirt, villagers fled to doorways and huddled behind the well's stone lip, the sky above darkened by a huge red-scaled shape circling low with wingbeats stirring dust across the square, cottage-eave shadows thrown long and flickering orange from reflected firelight above.
