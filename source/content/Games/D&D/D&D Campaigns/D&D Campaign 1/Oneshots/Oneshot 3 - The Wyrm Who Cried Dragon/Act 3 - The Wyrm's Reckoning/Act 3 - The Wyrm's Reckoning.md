Whatever the party decided to do with Nix — expose him to the village, help him keep his scheme running on fairer terms, or something in between — his mother has been searching the hills for weeks and finally traces him to Thistlewick. This act is the true climax: not a straightforward fight, but a tense, high-stakes negotiation with a very real adult dragon whose fury is really grief in disguise, resolved very differently depending on the choices the party carries into it.

---

## Scene 1 — Ignathra's Arrival

> [!tip] DM Cue
> This should feel like the stakes just went up an order of magnitude after two comedic-tinged fights. Play Ignathra's threat as completely real, but keep the path to a peaceful resolution genuinely open — this encounter is won with answers, not swords.
