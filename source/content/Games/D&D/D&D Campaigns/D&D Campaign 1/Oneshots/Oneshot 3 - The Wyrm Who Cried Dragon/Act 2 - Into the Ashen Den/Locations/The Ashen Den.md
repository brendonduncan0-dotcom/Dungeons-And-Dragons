## Notable Features

- Soot fans around the cave mouth, radiating outward in a pattern too small and too low to match an ancient dragon's scale
- A pile of discarded tribute — mostly food and simple goods, oddly little actual treasure for a supposed dragon's hoard
- Fresh boot prints crossing older, smaller clawed tracks near the entrance

## Hidden Details

- **DC 14 Perception:** The flickering light deeper in the cave pulses at irregular intervals, more like a torch or campfire than a magical or draconic glow.
- **DC 15 Investigation (boot prints):** The boot prints are recent, made by at least three different people moving fast and deliberately toward the cave's interior — someone else is already heading for the hoard.

> [!tip] DM Note
> This is the location for the Cinderhand Company race — if the party lingers investigating clues, the scavengers get further ahead and the encounter starts already in progress deeper in. If the party moves fast, they can catch the scavengers at the entrance instead. Either way, don't let the delay cost the party the fight entirely; it should always be catchable.
