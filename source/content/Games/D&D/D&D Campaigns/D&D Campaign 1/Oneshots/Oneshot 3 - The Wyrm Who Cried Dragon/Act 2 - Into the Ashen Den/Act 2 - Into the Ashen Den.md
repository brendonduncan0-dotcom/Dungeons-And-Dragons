The party follows the tribute road into the hills and reaches the dragon's lair — only to find a rival crew of treasure scavengers already trying to loot it. This act escalates from a race against opportunists to the central reveal of the whole one-shot: "Vhalzareth the Undying" is a fiction, and the truth waiting behind the illusion sets up the moral choice the rest of the session hinges on.

---

## Scene 1 — The Race for the Den

> [!tip] DM Cue
> The Cinderhand scavengers aren't true believers — they're here to loot what they assume is an unguarded hoard. Let the party choose whether this becomes a fight, a deal, or a race.

## Scene 2 — The Reveal

> [!tip] DM Cue
> This is the fire-breath moment: let the illusion be genuinely intimidating first, then let it crack. The party should walk away from this scene realizing the "ancient dragon" barely singed them compared to what the legend implied — and now has to decide what to do about that.
