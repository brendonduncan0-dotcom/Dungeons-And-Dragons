## Notable Features

- The "hoard" — mostly food, cloth, and simple tools, with only a thin scattering of actual coin
- A modest bonfire, clearly tended by hand, not any kind of magical brazier
- Deep claw gouges in the stone near the back wall — small, not the gouges of anything ancient

## Hidden Details

- **DC 13 Investigation (the hoard itself):** The "treasure" is almost entirely non-valuable goods — food, blankets, tools. An ancient dragon's hoard this thin doesn't add up, no matter how remote the lair.
- **DC 15 Perception (the voice):** There's a fractional delay between the booming voice and the shape moving behind the firelight — consistent with a sound illusion layered slightly out of sync with its source.

> [!tip] DM Note
> This is the reveal location — see [[Audience with the Ancient]] for the full beat-by-beat. The booming illusioned voice and towering shadow should feel genuinely intimidating on first read; the mismatch clues here are for players actively investigating, not something to hand over for free. Once the illusion breaks (whether from damage, a failed bluff, or Nix's own panic), describe the transformation vividly — the towering shadow flickering and dissolving to reveal the actual wyrmling underneath.
