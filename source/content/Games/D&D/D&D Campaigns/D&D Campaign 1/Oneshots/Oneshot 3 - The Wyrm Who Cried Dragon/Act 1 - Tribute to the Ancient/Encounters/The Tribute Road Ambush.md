**Act:** 1 — [[Act 1 - Tribute to the Ancient]]
**Location:** [[Thistlewick Outskirts]]
**Encounter type:** Combat

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Ahead, a farmer's cart lies tipped on its side, grain spilling across the road. Small hunched shapes in ragged armor dart between the hedgerows, and a reedy voice barks orders in Draconic: "Grab it all! Vhalzareth demands ONLY the finest tribute — and I will be the one to deliver it!"

## Setup
Grix and his kobold band have ambushed a farmer's tribute cart, intending to steal it and deliver it to the lair themselves for glory in their god's eyes. The farmer is cornered but not yet badly hurt. A second group of kobolds is circling to flank from the hedgerow (DC 13 Perception to spot before they act).

## Objectives
This encounter has more than one path to success:
- **Rescue the farmer** and drive off or defeat the kobolds
- **Capture Grix alive** for questioning — he's an easy (if absurd) source of intel confirming nobody has actually seen "Vhalzareth" up close
- **Simply escort the cart onward** if the party manages to scare off the kobolds without a full fight

## Enemies
| Creature | CR | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Grix]] | 1/2 | 1 | Leader; uses Rallying Cry, retreats if reduced below half HP with fewer than half his kobolds still standing |
| [[Kobold Tribute-Seeker]] | 1/8 | 4 | Fight in a cluster for Pack Tactics; scatter if Grix falls or flees |

**Terrain features:** The hedgerows provide the flanking kobolds cover until they emerge; the overturned cart offers cover for a defending PC or the farmer.

> [!warning] Scaling
> For a smaller or lower-level party, drop one Kobold Tribute-Seeker and have the flanking group announce itself with noise before acting (removing the surprise element). For a larger party, add a second flanking pair and have Grix hold back to direct rather than engage in melee immediately.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Farmer saved, kobolds defeated or driven off:** The farmer is deeply grateful and mentions, unprompted, that he's never actually seen more of the dragon than "a shadow and a lot of noise" — a free clue.
- **Grix captured:** He talks readily once cornered (see his NPC note) — comedic, contradictory, and ultimately confirms nobody in his band has ever seen Vhalzareth directly.
- **Kobolds allowed to flee with partial tribute:** The farmer loses some goods but survives; the party can still track the fleeing kobolds toward [[The Ashen Den]] if they choose.
