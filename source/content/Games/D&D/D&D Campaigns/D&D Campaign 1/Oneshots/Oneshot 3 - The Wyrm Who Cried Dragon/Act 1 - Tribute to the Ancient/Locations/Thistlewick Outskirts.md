## Notable Features

- The overturned cart, wheel still spinning — this just happened
- A terrified farmer pressed flat against the hedge, trying not to be seen
- A crude hand-drawn scroll dropped in the road, depicting an absurd, chicken-like "dragon"

## Hidden Details

- **DC 13 Perception:** A second group of kobolds is circling through the hedgerow to flank from the north.
- **DC 12 Investigation (the dropped scroll):** The drawing is captioned in shaky Draconic: "Vhalzareth, as He Truly Appears (probably)" — clear evidence the kobolds are guessing, not reporting firsthand knowledge.

> [!tip] DM Note
> Play this as tense-but-comedic — real danger to the farmer, but Grix's band is disorganized and more zealous than competent. If the party moves fast, they can save the farmer before any real harm; if they're slow, the farmer takes a hit or two but survives regardless (per campaign standards, no unavoidable death here). This is the first hard comedic beat undercutting "ancient dragon."
