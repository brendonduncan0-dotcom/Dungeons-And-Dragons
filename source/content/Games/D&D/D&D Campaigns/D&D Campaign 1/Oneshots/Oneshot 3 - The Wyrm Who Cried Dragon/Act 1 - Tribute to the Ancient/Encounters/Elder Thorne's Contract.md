**Act:** 1 — [[Act 1 - Tribute to the Ancient]]
**Location:** [[Thistlewick Village Square]]
**Encounter type:** Social

> [!info] Read Aloud
> A grey-haired woman with a farmer's hands steps out from the largest building, wiping flour from her palms before offering one to shake. "You're the guild's answer to my letter, I take it. Good. Walk with me — I'd rather not shout about dragons where the whole square can hear."

## Setup
Elder Maribel Thorne has summoned the guild to deal with "Vhalzareth the Undying," a dragon Thistlewick has been paying monthly tribute to for two months to avoid its wrath. She has never seen it up close herself — almost nobody has — but she's not willing to gamble the village's harvest on that uncertainty. She offers the job plainly and is willing to answer follow-up questions honestly, including revealing her scar if pressed (see her Secret).

## Objectives
Secure the contract, and learn as much as possible about the "dragon" before heading out — what villagers have actually witnessed, when tribute is collected, and where the tribute road leads.

## NPC Goals
Maribel wants the tribute to stop without her village burning for it. She's not attached to any particular method — combat, negotiation, or trickery are all fine by her, and she says so if asked. She does not want the party to provoke something they can't handle; if the party seems eager to charge in swinging, she'll caution them to at least scout first.

## Skill Challenge

| Check | DC | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persuasion or Insight | 13 | Maribel admits the scar on her arm isn't from a cookfire — she was singed by a disappointingly small blast of flame when she scouted the tribute road herself | She sticks to the "cookfire" story and the party leaves without this clue |
| History or Investigation | 12 | A villager recalls the tribute demands began abruptly two months ago, with no prior rumors of any dragon in the region before that | No additional context gained |

## Possible Outcomes
- **Contract accepted:** Maribel offers 75 gp per party member plus reasonable expenses, paid from village funds, for making the tribute problem go away by whatever means the party judges best. The party heads to the [[Thistlewick Outskirts]] toward the tribute road.
- **Party presses for more detail:** Maribel is forthcoming and has nothing to hide — this just yields more of the exploration clues above, no complication attached.
