## Tactics
These kobolds fight in a tight cluster around Grix to keep Pack Tactics active, target the most exposed PC first, and flee toward the tree line the moment Grix falls or calls a retreat. They are not fanatics willing to die for the cause — a clearly losing fight or a captured, questioned Grix is enough to make the survivors scatter rather than fight to the last. None of them have ever seen "Vhalzareth" and none will claim otherwise if questioned; they'll cheerfully describe wildly inconsistent, secondhand mental images of their "god."
