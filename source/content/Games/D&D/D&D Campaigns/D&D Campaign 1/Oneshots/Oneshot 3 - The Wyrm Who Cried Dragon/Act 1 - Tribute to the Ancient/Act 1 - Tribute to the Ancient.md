The party arrives in Thistlewick to find a village organizing its entire calendar around monthly tribute to "Vhalzareth the Undying," a dragon almost nobody has actually seen up close. This act is about establishing the absurdity and real fear of the situation, taking the guild contract, and planting the first seeds of doubt that the threat isn't what it's rumored to be — before a kobold ambush on the tribute road turns those doubts into an active, if comedic, fight.

---

## Scene 1 — The Contract

> [!tip] DM Cue
> Play the village's fear as genuine even as the details start to feel a little too convenient — nobody has really seen this dragon, and that should nag at the players before they're told to think about it.
