**Act:** 2 — [[Act 2 - Into the Ashen Den]]
**Location:** [[Vhalzareth's Hoard Chamber]]
**Encounter type:** Combat (with a strong social off-ramp)

> [!info] Read Aloud
> **"WHO DARES DISTURB VHALZARETH THE UNDYING?"** The voice booms from everywhere and nowhere, rattling loose stone from the ceiling. A towering shadow rears up behind the bonfire, wings spreading to fill the chamber — and then, a beat too long after the voice fades, something in the shadow flickers.

## Setup
This is the reveal encounter. Nix, wearing the Ring of Minor Illusions, is putting on his full performance — booming voice, towering illusory silhouette, maximum menace — to scare off whoever has intruded on his lair. He does not want a fight; he wants the intruders gone before they get close enough to see through the act. If the party pushes forward, questions him confidently, or attacks, the illusion is put under strain and eventually breaks.

## Objectives
- **Investigate the mismatch** between the voice/shadow and the modest hoard/claw marks (see [[Vhalzareth's Hoard Chamber]] hidden details) to gain advantage going in
- **Force or provoke the reveal** — through combat, a called bluff, or simply refusing to be intimidated — and then decide how to handle Nix once he's exposed
- **Survive the fire breath** if things go loud — it hits hard for a moment but is not close to the scale the illusion implied

## Enemies
| Creature | CR | Count | Notes |

**Terrain features:** See [[Vhalzareth's Hoard Chamber]] — the bonfire provides light but no real cover; the hoard pile can be used as difficult footing.

> [!warning] Scaling
> Nix is not trying to kill the party — he's trying to scare them off. If a PC drops to 0 HP, have him break off immediately (panic, not bloodlust) rather than pressing an advantage, consistent with the "always telegraphed, never a surprise TPK" table standard. For a smaller party, have his illusion break automatically after the first breath weapon use rather than requiring damage thresholds.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Party attacks immediately:** Nix responds with Fire Breath (his one big scare tactic) and then, illusion broken and badly outmatched, drops the act entirely and either flees, surrenders, or begs for a deal depending on how the party responds to seeing how small he actually is.
- **Party investigates or calls the bluff without attacking:** Nix's nerve breaks under direct, confident questioning (DC 14 Intimidation or Insight-informed Persuasion) and he reveals himself voluntarily, launching straight into pleading his case (see [[Nix]]'s Motivation and Secret).
- **Party plays along or negotiates first:** Nix, relieved not to be immediately attacked, is receptive to a real conversation about ending or adjusting the scheme — this is the doorway into the Act 3 choice.

Regardless of path, this encounter should end with Nix's true identity known to the party and the central question of the one-shot on the table: expose him, or help him keep the con going on better terms. Track which the party leans toward — it determines how [[Ignathra's Return]] plays out.
