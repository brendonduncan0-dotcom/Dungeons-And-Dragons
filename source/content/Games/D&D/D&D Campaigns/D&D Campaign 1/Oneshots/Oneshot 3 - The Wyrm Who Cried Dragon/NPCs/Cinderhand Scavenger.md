## Tactics
The Cinderhand Company scouts prioritize speed over bloodshed — their goal is reaching the hoard first, not killing the party. They'll use Stealth to slip past the party where possible, fight defensively to buy time for their crew to advance if cornered, and offer a split of the hoard or a retreat if a fight is clearly going against them. They break and run rather than fight to the death; none of them are interested in dying over someone else's dragon's treasure.
