**Act:** 2 — [[Act 2 - Into the Ashen Den]]
**Location:** [[The Ashen Den]]
**Encounter type:** Combat

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Voices echo ahead in the cave passage — not draconic, human. "Careful, careful — if this thing really is as old as they say, one whiff of us and we're ash. Grab what's near the entrance and go." Three figures crouch over the discarded tribute pile, stuffing goods into sacks.

## Setup
A crew from the Cinderhand Company — opportunistic treasure scavengers, not true believers in the "ancient dragon" — heard the tribute rumors and decided to loot what they assume is an undefended, or at least distracted, dragon's hoard before anyone official gets to it. They are not looking for a fight with the party; they're looking to grab what they can and leave before the "dragon" notices.

## Objectives
Multiple valid approaches:
- **Fight them off** to secure the path (and the actual hoard) for the party
- **Negotiate a split or a head start** — the scavengers will take a deal that lets them leave with something rather than risk a fight
- **Let them race ahead** and use the resulting noise/distraction to the party's advantage when approaching Nix afterward

## Enemies
| Creature | CR | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Cinderhand Scavenger]] | 1/2 | 3 | Prioritize escape over the fight; will offer a split of loot if cornered |

**Terrain features:** See [[The Ashen Den]] — the sloping passage and loose scree make Stealth and Dexterity-based repositioning easy for the scavengers.

> [!warning] Scaling
> For a smaller or lower-level party, reduce to 2 Scavengers who are actively trying to flee rather than fight. For a larger party, add a fourth Scavenger and have them attempt to bar the passage with an overturned crate rather than fight directly.

## Possible Outcomes
- **Scavengers defeated or driven off:** The party proceeds to [[Vhalzareth's Hoard Chamber]] uncontested, and can search the scavengers for a rough hand-drawn map of the tribute road (no mechanical benefit, just flavor confirming they were guessing at the layout too).
- **Deal struck:** The scavengers take a portion of the discarded tribute (not the true hoard) and leave peacefully, mentioning they never actually got a look at anything resembling a real dragon before the party arrived.
- **Scavengers allowed to go first:** They stumble into the [[Audience with the Ancient]] encounter ahead of the party, giving the party a chance to observe Nix's illusion trick used on someone else first — a strong hint before the party has to decide how to approach.
