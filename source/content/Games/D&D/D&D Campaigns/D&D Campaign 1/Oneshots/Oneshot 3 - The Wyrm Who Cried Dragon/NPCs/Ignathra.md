**Role:** Climax NPC — genuine threat and grieving parent
**First appears:** Act 3
**Location:** [[Thistlewick Village Square]]

## Personality
- Imperious and quick to anger, as befits a young adult red dragon — but the anger is a mask over real fear for her missing hatchling
- Contemptuous of the "Vhalzareth" name and reputation the moment she hears it — she knows exactly how young and small her son actually is, and hearing a village call him "ancient" and "undying" reads to her as either mockery or a trap
- Capable of being reasoned with, but only by someone who leads with Nix's safety, not with excuses about the tribute scheme

## Appearance
Scales the deep red of banked coals, easily four times Nix's length, with old scarring along one wing membrane from a territorial fight years ago.

## Motivation
Ignathra has been quartering the hills for weeks searching for Nix after he fled the lair. She wants her son found, safe, and home — everything else (the village, the tribute, the guild's involvement) is noise to her unless it touches that goal. She is not interested in punishing Thistlewick for tribute paid to what they believed was a real ancient dragon; she is very interested in whoever put her son in danger, whether that's framed as "the village exploited him" or "the village threatened him," depending on what she's told.

## Secret
Ignathra already suspects Nix is behind the "ancient dragon" rumors — young reds are notorious for exactly this kind of overreaching bravado — and is more relieved than furious to have it confirmed, provided she's given any reason at all to believe he's unharmed. Her rage is a negotiating posture as much as a genuine threat. A party that opens with proof Nix is alive and unhurt (bringing him out, or convincing evidence) can defuse most of this encounter before it becomes a fight. A party that arrives without an answer for "where is my son" gets the fight instead.

## DM Note — Running This Encounter

> [!warning] This is a negotiation with teeth, not a boss fight
> Ignathra's stat block is here for reference and to make the threat real, but this encounter is designed to be resolved primarily through roleplay — see the [[Ignathra's Return]] encounter note for the full skill challenge and off-ramps. She should posture, use Frightful Presence, and threaten convincingly, but should not open with her breath weapon unless the party attacks first or the negotiation collapses completely. Always telegraph clearly before this becomes a real fight — per table standards, no PC should go from full HP to dying without a clear warning beat first.
