    Goblin Warlord

    Small humanoid (goblinoid), neutral evil

    Armor Class 15 (studded leather)

    Hit Points 91 (14d6 + 42)

    Speed 30 ft.

    STRDEXCONINTWISCHA
    10 (+0)16 (+3)16 (+3)18 (+4)12 (+1)14 (+2)

    Saving Throws Dex +6, Int +7, Wis +4

    Skills Intimidation +5, Perception +4, Stealth +6

    Damage Resistances psychic

    Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14

    Languages Common, Goblin, Deep Speech

    Challenge 5

    Nimble Escape. The warlord can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

    Psychic Bulwark. The warlord has advantage on saving throws against being Charmed or Frightened.

    Warlord's Presence. Goblinoid allies within 30 ft. of the warlord that can see or hear it gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and saving throws.

    Actions

    Multiattack. The warlord makes two Psionic Blade attacks, or one Psionic Blade attack and one Psychic Lash.

    Psionic Blade. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) psychic damage.

    Psychic Lash. Ranged Spell Attack: +7 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (4d6 + 4) psychic damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened of the warlord until the end of its next turn.

    Mind Shatter (Recharge 5-6). The warlord targets one creature it can see within 60 ft. The target must make a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 35 (10d6) psychic damage and is Stunned until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, the target takes half damage and is not Stunned.

    Reactions

    Psionic Shield. When the warlord would take damage, it reduces that damage by 11 (2d10). If this reduces the damage to 0, the attacker takes 5 (1d10) psychic damage.

    Lore

    Goblin warlords gifted with psionic power are rare aberrations in goblinoid society — feared even by their own kind. These individuals developed their psychic abilities through exposure to aberrant energies: proximity to a mind flayer colony, a Far Realm rift seeping into their warren, or extended contact with a stolen illithid artifact. Where ordinary goblin chieftains rule through cunning and brute fear, a Goblin Warlord holds its tribe in an iron psychic grip, implanting suggestions directly into followers’ minds and punishing treachery with crippling mental agony.

    Within the tribe, the warlord is regarded as touched by dark gods — something beyond mortal comprehension. Followers obey not merely out of habit but because dissent carries a physical cost. As a result, a tribe under a Goblin Warlord’s control is unnervingly disciplined compared to the rabbles other adventurers expect. The warlord also speaks Deep Speech, a tongue it likely learned from the aberrant sources of its power, and it uses this knowledge to negotiate — or make threats — in circles far above the typical goblin’s station.

    Goblin Warlords are deeply ambitious. They seek out further sources of aberrant power to grow their psionic gifts, and many dream of uniting disparate goblin tribes under one psychically enforced banner. A few have come dangerously close to succeeding. They are calculating enemies who never spend a minion’s life wastefully — every goblin is a chess piece, and the warlord is always three moves ahead.

    Tactics

    The warlord avoids direct melee and opens combat from range, using Psychic Lash to Frighten the most dangerous-looking opponent before it can close the distance. Warlord’s Presence passively buffs all goblin allies on the field — the warlord maintains line of sight to as many minions as possible, so positioning matters. Mind Shatter is saved for the character who looks most capable of reaching the warlord (the heavily armoured warrior, the aggressive paladin), deployed the moment it recharges.

    When threatened in melee, the warlord uses Psionic Shield to blunt incoming blows, then uses its Nimble Escape bonus action to Hide and slip back to a ranged position. Psychic Command is used liberally to direct minions into flanking positions, or to trigger a free attack from a minion already engaged with a distracted player. If reduced below half HP, the warlord psychically commands its fastest goblin to make a suicidal charge into the most dangerous enemy as a distraction, then attempts to flee — it knows no war is won from a corpse.

    Encounter Ideas

    The Mind-Locked Tribe: The party enters a goblin warren and finds something unsettling — the goblins are silent, coordinated, their eyes occasionally going blank mid-sentence. The whole tribe is under the warlord’s psychic grip. A few goblins are dimly aware that something is wrong but are too controlled to act. If the players can reach and defeat the warlord, the tribe’s conditioning may collapse — leaving a confused, traumatised rabble who were victims as much as enemies.

    Siege at Stillwater Bridge: A goblin force has blockaded the only river crossing for miles, led by the warlord directing from a fortified hilltop. It uses Mind Shatter to disable whoever tries to lead the assault, and Psychic Command keeps goblin archers in a devastating coordinated volley. The party must find a way to reach the warlord through the chaotic press below — perhaps flanking through the river, or using the terrain to break line of sight and deny its Warlord’s Presence buff.

    The Warlord’s Offer: The Goblin Warlord approaches the party under a flag of truce with a grudging proposal: something more powerful has moved into the region — a mind flayer outpost, an undead warlord — and is interfering with its own ambitions. It wants a temporary alliance. The warlord is utterly untrustworthy and the players know it. But the common enemy is real. What do they do with a villain who genuinely wants to help them this once?
