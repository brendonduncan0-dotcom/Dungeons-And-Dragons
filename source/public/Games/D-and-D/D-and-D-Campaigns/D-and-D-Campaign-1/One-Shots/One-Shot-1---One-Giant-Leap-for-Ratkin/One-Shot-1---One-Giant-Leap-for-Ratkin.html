    One-Shot 1 - One Giant Leap for Ratkin

    Premise

    A lunar eclipse is hours away, and something is stirring in the merchant quarter. Specific supplies have gone missing from warehouses, and a trail of cryptic symbols has been scratched into walls across the district — marks that appear at wildly different ages, as if left by many hands over generations. The party has a contract to investigate. What they’ll find is a centuries-long rodent conspiracy, an underground city of true believers, and a ballista aimed at the moon — because the Moonrats are this close to bringing down the giant wheel of cheese in the sky.

    Hook

    The adventuring guild has received a complaint from Aldric Thane, a merchant whose import warehouse has been systematically stripped of rope, copper wire, and timber — only those materials, nothing of obvious value. Stranger still: whoever took them left symbols scratched near every entry point they used.

    Alternative hooks:

    • A city guard contact (Sergeant Wenda Brix) asks the party to look into the graffiti plaguing the merchant quarter, which the city can’t spare resources to investigate.
    • A party member notices a rat watching them with unusual intensity — following them across rooftops — and decides to find out why.

    Act 1 — The Rat Report

    The party receives the contract at the guild and meets Aldric Thane, who is equal parts baffled and offended. Investigation of his warehouse reveals the robbery was conducted with unusual precision: entry points are small (rodent-sized), specific materials were taken, and symbols are scratched near each hole. Thane doesn’t believe it was rats. He is wrong.

    Following the symbols outside leads into the merchant quarter proper. The marks aren’t random graffiti — they form a trail. The party must decode enough of the symbol system to follow it before dark.

    Key scenes: The Guild Contract · Thane’s Import House Investigation · Decoding the Scratched Walls

    Act 2 — The Warrens Below

    The trail leads to an entrance beneath the Coppergate Mill: a brick tunnel, rodent-sized but recently widened. Inside, the Great Warren unfolds — an underground complex that clearly took decades to construct. Chambers include sleeping areas, a food cache, and most importantly: the Great Archive, where generations of Moonrat scholars have carved their history, plans, and the sacred Cheese Moon Doctrine into every available surface.

    The party can piece together the full picture here. The eclipse is tonight. Their predecessors built most of the ballista over multiple eclipse cycles. Tonight’s cohort plans to fire it. The machine was hauled up to the Coppergate Mill roof in pieces the night before. Moonrat guards must be dealt with to get through. A hidden alcove in the archive contains the generational treasure hoard — trinkets and coins collected over centuries.

    Key scenes: Into the Warren

    Act 3 — Rooftop Reckoning

    The eclipse begins. The party reaches the mill roof to find three Moonrat Sappers assembling the final components of the ballista while Grand Squeaker Nibblefang XLII — moments ago an ordinary rat — straightens up, adjusts a tiny sash, and begins addressing the party in flawless Common.

    The party can fight, sabotage the ballista outright, or negotiate with Nibblefang, who is intelligent, eloquent, and completely wrong about the moon being cheese. The eclipse lasts about 10 minutes of in-game time. When it ends, the Grand Squeaker trails off mid-sentence and wanders toward a drainpipe, confused.

    Climax: Rooftop Reckoning

    Possible Endings

    • Full Victory: The party disables the ballista and either defeats or talks down the Moonrats before the eclipse ends. The merchant quarter is safe. Thane pays up. The moon remains intact.
    • Partial Victory: The ballista fires — but the party damaged it enough that the bolt falls short and lands in the river. No one is hurt. The eclipse ends and the rats disperse. Thane still pays, albeit grumpily, as his stolen materials are a total loss.
    • Pyrrhic: The ballista fires true. The bolt strikes the moon… and bounces off. The moon is made of rock. The Moonrats are devastated. The Grand Squeaker, mid-eclipse, weeps. The party may never let this session be forgotten.

    DM Notes

    Pacing: This one-shot runs in roughly three hours. Act 1 (30–40 min), Act 2 (60–75 min including combat), Act 3 (30–40 min). Don’t let the symbol-decoding drag — give partial information after two successes so the party always feels progress.

    The Eclipse Timer: Once the party reaches the rooftop, the eclipse begins. Track it loosely — end it when dramatically appropriate, not on a rigid round count. The moment it ends mid-Nibblefang-sentence is the payoff.

    Grand Squeaker as a Social Encounter: Nibblefang is not evil. He is a genius rat who genuinely believes in the Cheese Moon and has devoted his brief intelligent life to this moment. He will listen to reason but will not accept “the moon is made of rock” without extraordinary evidence. He will argue philosophy. He will quote his ancestors. Lean into it.

    Common Derailments:

    • Party tries to keep a moonrat as a pet: Sure — a normal rat is a normal rat 23 hours a day.
    • Party tries to collapse the warren: Too extensive to collapse quickly. It would take days of sustained effort.
    • Party waits out the eclipse rather than engaging: The ballista is already aimed and loaded. If the party does nothing, it fires. Nibblefang anticipated this and has a fuse mechanism on the trigger.
